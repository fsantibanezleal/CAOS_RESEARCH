## Smale, problem 6 (CCB-011)

Source: S. Smale, "Mathematical Problems for the Next Century", Second Version, dated 7 August 1998 (author's text,
hosted at https://www.fim.uni-passau.de/fileadmin/dokumente/fakultaeten/fim/lehrstuhl/muller/SmaleProblems1998.pdf),
archived under `caos-research/central-configurations/sources/ccb011/` (SHA-256
93febbea87721f4f88464aba32fa1399c63fa1a2ee1f9789a983ee1a3e304e81). The Intelligencer printing (20 (1998), no. 2,
7-15) is not read; its wording may differ from this second version.

Verbatim [V]: "Problem 6: Finiteness of the number of relative equilibria in celestial mechanics. Is the number of
relative equilibria finite, in the n-body problem of celestial mechanics, for any choice of positive real numbers
m1, ..., mn as the masses?" Smale adds that the problem is in Wintner's book (1941), that a relative equilibrium is
a solution of Newton's equations induced by a plane rotation, that for 4 bodies finiteness is unknown (as of 1998),
and gives the equivalent form: for any choice of m1, ..., mn, does the function induced by the potential on
(S - Delta)/SO(2) have a finite number of critical points? He cites Shub (1970) for compactness of the critical set.

## Roberts, the continuum with one negative mass (CCB-006)

Source: G. E. Roberts, "A Continuum of Relative Equilibria in the 5-Body Problem", preprint mp_arc 98-554
(6 August 1998), https://web.ma.utexas.edu/mp_arc-bin/mpa?yn=98-554 (PostScript, converted to PDF for reading),
archived under `caos-research/central-configurations/sources/ccb006/` (98-554.ps.gz SHA-256
320a753e447661285ec86aeffe7e86c50942e35d35cafad4580adbb402518b27). Published as Physica D 127 (1999) 141-145; the
journal version is not read.

[V], Section 3 (signs checked on the rendered page): four bodies on a rhombus, masses m1 = 1, m2 = m, m3 = 1,
m4 = m, and a central body of mass m5 = p. The equations reduce to omega^2 = 2m/c^3 + 1/4 + p and
m(2/c^3 - 1/(4k^3)) + p(1 - 1/k^3) = 2/c^3 - 1/4 (equation (3)). The pair (m, p) = (1, -1/4) makes (3)
degenerate. Theorem 3.1: for the masses (1, 1, 1, 1, -1/4) there is a one-parameter family of degenerate relative
equilibria, the four equal masses at the vertices of a rhombus of fixed side and the fifth body at the centre.
Corollary 3.2: for these masses the number of relative equilibrium classes is not finite. The continuum ends at
configurations where three bodies collide; the potential stays constant along it because the negative mass cancels
the forces. Exact negative mass: -1/4 (the [U] in our references is lifted).
