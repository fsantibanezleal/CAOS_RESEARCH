## Fifteen defects found and fixed

Thirteen in the manuscript and notes:
1. **134,497 against 134,494** for the same quantity. My depth-6 count included
   the three FREE INPUTS (-1), (1) and x. This is the identical artifact EXP-003
   hit one depth down (11,380 against 11,377) and wrote into its verdict; I
   reintroduced a documented bug. Results unaffected: the extras have degrees
   0, 1, 0.
2. A gap in the zpmax upper-bound proof: mu(a)+mu(b)+1 does not close the
   induction when sub-DAGs are shared. Fixed to distinct-gate counting with
   max+1.
3. "The minimal-gate root sets are intervals": FALSE. {+-1,+-2} at tau=5 and
   {-1,1} at tau=2 are records and not intervals.
4. "Both counts are tight", which reads as claiming optimality the scan has not
   yet established.
5. Chebyshev comparison claimed four real roots at five gates; the tower is at
   two there (T_2 costs 3 gates, T_4 costs 6).
6. Abstract said the real records "are generated by" the doubling step; the step
   links tau=4 to tau=6 only.
7. The zero-polynomial exclusion was stated without its degree side-condition;
   unconditional discard would drop the high-degree witnesses the scan targets.
8-10. Three mutually inconsistent refutation counts (two, three, four) in three
   places; reconciled to five structural plus two tractability, one partial.
11-13. Data section: "24 cores" (it is 32), "under twenty hours" of compute
   (actually ~40 h plus a multi-day scan), "EXP-001--EXP-005" (now 014),
   standard-library-only arithmetic (NumPy and SymPy now used, exactly, and now
   disclosed), frontier "28 GB" (27.3 GiB).

Two in the validation tooling itself, both caught by testing the tests:
14. The consistency gate's first pattern could not match the LaTeX form
    `134{,}497` at all, so its PASS was partly vacuous. It now self-tests
    against the exact pre-fix strings and refuses to report a verdict unless it
    proves it can detect them.
15. The ladder check keyed on numeric prefixes and cried wolf on four correct
    rows, because zmax, zqmax and zrmax all begin 1,2,3 and the doubling
    family's degree row begins 4,8,16 like its real row. Rewritten to key on
    each row's label.

A sixteenth was caught mid-audit: an EXP-011 completeness check read a `zmax`
key that the partition files do not have, so it silently reported 0. The files
carry a histogram; reading the right field is what produced the definitive
zmax(8) = 6 confirmation above.
