# Trace-aware follow-up preflight

The October source review and EXP-013 cap identify the deciding constraint:
the clipped Gram defect must meet the block pressure lower bound without
making the off-line-pair residual negative. The original dichotomy uses
only one saturated eigenvalue, although every block has trace m.
Retaining the complementary zero-sum displacements is an invariant-first
test, with a complete analytic target stated in EXP-016 before computation.

The mechanism is elementary Jensen and the classical mean/variance control
of eigenvalues. It is not a priority claim for a new trace inequality.
Primary prior art: Wolkowicz and Styan, *Bounds for eigenvalues using traces*,
Linear Algebra and its Applications 29 (1980), 471-506,
doi:10.1016/0024-3795(80)90258-X. The author-hosted paper's trace/variance
sections were inspected; it is background, not an analytic zeta premise.
[Author copy](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~hwolkowi/henry/reports/PAPER31.pdf).

No overlap for the proposed clipped-block substitution was located in the
bounded search for the source identifier and trace/clipping/distinct-zero
terms. This does not prove novelty. The local certificate and analytic
inputs are identical to EXP-013. A small arithmetic consequence stays in
the research record, with no new standalone paper or Zenodo deposit.
