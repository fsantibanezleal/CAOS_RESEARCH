# Reciprocity and compact public evidence recheck

Primary sources are archived and hashed in source-manifest-exp021-reciprocity.json.
The Bettin paper is arXiv:1607.05595v1, with its TeX source; the Devine
record is [Zenodo 22066689](https://zenodo.org/records/22066689), including
its PDF and compressed public posting. These older records are not new
discoveries or new October releases. The Devine claim was already tracked
in the September 26/27 dossiers.

Bettin's [Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.05595) gives a meromorphic
relation between shifted Dirichlet moments and Estermann functions. The
following remark explicitly permits a non-prime-modulus generalization;
its displayed prime formula still has to be replaced by complete gcd and
conductor terms. EXP-021 provides that classical arithmetic bookkeeping.
Theorem 5 is an exact identity, not a uniform short-window averaged
asymptotic at polynomial height. The reciprocal expansions, remainder
regularity and their parameter dependence need separate examination.
Theorem 5 and its proof were directly read in the archived TeX/PDF; no
claim is made here to have validated every theorem in the 23-page paper.

An exact Gaussian-Mellin representation is another possible starting
point. On s=1/2+it write G(s)=exp((s-1/2-iT)^2/H^2) and use
zeta(1-s+beta)=2(2*pi)^(-(s-beta))*Gamma(s-beta)
cos(pi*(s-beta)/2)*zeta(s-beta). Moving to an absolutely convergent line
requires the actual residues, including the cancellation at s=1+beta.
A Mellin-Gaussian convolution can keep the full cos(2*pi*x*exp(2y/H))
phase. This sketch is unverified ongoing mathematics, not a theorem or
an improved moment range. No control computation has been run on it.
It does not remove the signed-summation obligation by itself.

Devine's public PDF states 0.673399 for simple critical zeros through
four profiles and a range-5 subaction. The six-entry compressed archive
was checked against its published SHA-256 and read without executing
external code. Its README and release policy explicitly identify the
6052-byte certificate core as a contract/identity summary and say that
the full numerical replay package remains in preparation. The archive
does not contain the profile coefficients, complete transition domains,
verifier or numerical payloads needed for independent full replay.
The claim is retained as an unrefereed external result, not upgraded by
downloading its summary. Reading concentrated on its first five sections,
certificate/finite-word interfaces and release policy; its separate
conditional horizontal-profile theorems have not been fully audited here.

Multi-profile averaging, coboundaries, renewal ledgers and pressure
optimization are established prior mechanisms. EXP-022's new scoped
output is the exact two-configuration pressure-family obstruction for
the particular nine-point distinct-strip application. No priority for
those general mechanisms is asserted. The proposed changed-pressure
lower bound needs a separately declared universal certificate.
