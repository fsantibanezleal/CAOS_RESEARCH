## Higher and mixed moments

[Zeta Lab](https://github.com/teal-sea/zeta-lab) at
a9f0b38b7eb68435b4d2e8dc693f2c5d3d3d0a03 has current cycle-moment derivations
and counterexamples. The inspected finite theorem extends the adapted-subspace
count with globally concave spectral scores. A quartic perturbation requires
a matched fourth-moment bound. Its signed-pair construction demonstrates why
a favorable third discrepancy alone is insufficient. The mixed-observer
version instead uses a narrow fourth moment, but the reported tested profiles
give a negative numerator. These are useful finite methods with open analytic
obligations, not an established stronger zeta proportion.

The original [Rudnick--Sarnak paper](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~rudnick/papers/nlevelDuke.pdf)
was checked directly on pages 284--285. Theorem 3.1 has smooth entire height
weights and strict Fourier-support restrictions; Theorem 3.2 assumes RH for
its unsmoothed counterpart. Replacing the counting operator by a differently
weighted one therefore needs its own proof. Our exact finite preflight in
2026-10-03-weighted-cycle-bridge-preflight.md rejects two naive substitutions,
including a squared-weight normalization attempt. Its independent rational
diagonal calculation agrees with symbolic vector algebra. No actual zeta
configuration is constructed and neither published theorem is contradicted.
