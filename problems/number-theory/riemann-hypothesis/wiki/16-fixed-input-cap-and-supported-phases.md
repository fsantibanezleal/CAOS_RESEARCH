## Pointwise phase resolution on mollifier support

EXP-014 constructs exact collisions hm-kn=1 at the approximate-functional-
equation product scale. EXP-015 makes h,k squarefree and strictly below
the mollifier cutoff, with nonzero coefficients for P(x)=x.
For every M>=1000000 such consecutive twists exist between M/2 and 3M/4.
For T=16(Mr)^2, m=kr+1,n=hr+1, the phase satisfies

    H/(2M^2 r) <= H log(hm/kn) <= 5H/(M^2 r).

With M=B^b,r=B^(a-b),H=B^d this has exponent d-a-b,
whose zero is nu=theta-1/2. The obstruction to uniform integration by
parts therefore survives actual basic mollifier support. Signed sums may
still cancel; there is no new zero proportion or moment refutation.
