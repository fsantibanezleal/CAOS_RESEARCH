## What is established, and by what

1. The reading of the last step is confirmed numerically: the transform of S is -zeta'/zeta(s) * 1/(s (s - 1)), the
   subtracted terms are x log x - (1 + gamma) x, the constant term is log 2 pi and the trivial zeros contribute
   the sum of x^(-2k)/(2k (2k + 1)). Formula (2) of the note agrees with the direct computation at 460 points to
   within 5 percent of its truncation bound. [finite agreement; the proof is in the note]
2. Under the Riemann hypothesis the sum G stays within 0.0462 sqrt(x) of log 2 pi; up to 10^8 it uses about half
   of that, with both signs. The bound the formal proof supplies, G(x) <= x^(1/2 + epsilon) on windows, is
   therefore weak in size and one-sided; its difficulty is that it is proved at all.
3. The identity 2 + gamma - log 4 pi for the sum of 1/|rho|^2 is reproduced to 2e-10 from the zeros and the
   density of the remaining ones.

## Deviations from the declaration

- The zeros. The declared table (Odlyzko, `zeros1`) could not be fetched: its host did not present a certificate
  this device accepts, and the download was not forced. The first 100,000 zeros were taken from the LMFDB list
  (`https://www.lmfdb.org/zeros/zeta/list?limit=100000&N=1`, 31 to 37 digits, indices checked consecutive; file
  SHA-256 `af8ca55f...451f1` as downloaded, `b3bc6126...dc7e6` as the column of ordinates). Gate G3 was declared on
  the values, which are met. A local computation with certified balls (Arb) was started as a second source and
  stopped after 25 minutes without a result on the shared machine; it is not part of this verdict.
- P2. The first run reported a failure: its test compared, on each unit interval [N, N + 1], the largest
  deviation with K sqrt(N) + (1 - log 2)/(N + 1)^2, which is sufficient but too coarse where the last term
  matters, and it failed at N = 1 (margin -0.138) although the bound itself holds there (at x = 1 the deviation is
  0.2607 and the bound 0.3531). The test was refined to 4,096 pieces per unit interval for N < 1000; the run was
  repeated in full. No other number changed between the two runs. The first results file is kept.
- Time: 84 seconds on one process.

## How could this be wrong?

- The control shares the definition of G with the note; an error common to both (for instance in the reading of
  `Formalism.Gz`) would not be seen. The Lean definition was read directly: `Sz x` is the sum over n from 1 to
  floor(x) of Lambda(n)(x/n - 1) and `Gz x = Sz x - x log x + (1 + gamma) x`.
- The explicit formula is checked with zeros below height 74,921; a zero off the line at great height would change
  G by an amount invisible at x <= 10^8.
- Double precision loses about seven digits in G near 10^8; the 50-digit references bound the error by 5e-8 up to
  10^7, and the margins of P2 and P3 are larger by orders of magnitude.
