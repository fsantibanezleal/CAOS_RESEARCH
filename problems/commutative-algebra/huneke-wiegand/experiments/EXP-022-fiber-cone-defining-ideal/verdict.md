
## Validation interpretation

For each degree `d`, the program quotients all symmetric degree-`d` monomials by variable
multiples of the complete actual degree-`d-1` kernel. The excess of that quotient dimension over
`dim_k(C_p)_d` is exactly the number of new minimal equations in degree `d`. The degree-three
excess is one in all three complete runs; adding it leaves no degree-four or degree-five excess.

The finite agreement supports but does not prove that the displayed cubic is the only higher
minimal equation for every `p`. The source theorem bounds all remaining uncertainty to degrees
three through five.
