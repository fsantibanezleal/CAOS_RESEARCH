# EXP-022 - defining ideal of the conductor fiber cone

Status: DECLARED before implementation or execution on 2026-08-17.

## Question

For `p>=4`, is the defining ideal `J_p` of the conductor special fiber `C_p=F(T_p)` generated by
its quadratic value-congruence relations, and what is its exact first graded Betti row?

## Falsifiable predictions

- P1: `J_p` consists exactly of homogeneous value-congruence relations: zero monomials whose
  value sums lie in `m_pT_p^n`, and binomial differences between nonzero monomials with equal
  value sum.
- P2: the quadratic subideal `K_p=(J_p)_2S_p` equals `J_p` for every `p>=4`.
- P3: the only minimal defining equations are

```text
beta_(1,2)(C_p)=50p^2-17p,
beta_(1,j)(C_p)=0 for j=3,4,5 and every j>5.
```

- P4: after killing the parameter variable `X_(24p)`, the quadratic relations leave exactly
  `(1,10p-1,12p,2p-1,1)` standard classes in degrees zero through four and no degree-five class.

P1 is a semantic kernel description. P2--P4 are the decision-bearing conjecture and can fail.

## Premise dependencies

EXP-013 and EXP-016--017 supply the conductor values, powers, minimal reduction, and reduction
number. EXP-021 supplies Cohen--Macaulayness, the exact fiber and Artinian bases, and the value
criterion for zero products. Abdolmaleki--Kumashiro Theorem 2.8 supplies the independent upper
bound five on defining-equation degrees. A failed premise invalidates the deductive conclusion.

## Exact routes

- Route A: enumerate degree-`n` symmetric monomials in the `10p` minimal generators, partition
  them by value sum and zero/nonzero image, and compute the congruence induced by multiplying all
  lower-degree relations by variables.
- Route B: compute expected nonzero quotient dimensions from the closed EXP-021 basis blocks and
  independently check the degree-two kernel count.
- Audit: rebuild selected summaries without importing Route A's union-find state, rehash artifacts,
  and inject disconnected, false-zero, and false-binomial controls.

## Mandatory smoke and stop rules

Run `p=4` in degrees two through five, in order. Stop at the first degree where the quadratic
closure has more classes than `C_4`; that degree contains a genuine higher-degree minimal
relation and refutes P2/P3. Preserve a canonical witness consisting of two equal-image components
or a zero monomial disconnected from zero.

If the smoke run passes, test a bounded multi-parameter set before attempting a symbolic proof.
No finite campaign proves P2. If the five-minute or 2 GiB smoke budget is exceeded, the verdict is
`INCONCLUSIVE`, not failure.

## Confirmation rule

`CONFIRMED` requires: the semantic kernel proof; the source-backed degree-five completeness
argument; exact smoke and campaign agreement; independent audit and corruptions; and a symbolic
block/confluence proof valid for every `p>=4`. A computational pass alone is
`COMPUTATIONAL_PASS`.

## Adversarial controls

- remove one quadratic zero relation;
- split one equal-sum quadratic fiber;
- falsely connect monomials with distinct sums;
- falsely kill a known nonzero Artinian basis class;
- perturb `50p^2-17p` by one;
- suppress the first higher-degree witness if quadraticity fails.

Every corruption must be detected.

## Research lenses

- systematic spine: exact exclusion/adjudication by homogeneous degree;
- invariant lens: value sums and membership in `m_pT_p^n`;
- automaton lens: Artinian offset blocks and multiplication transitions;
- adversarial lens: disconnected congruence components and corrupted relations;
- source lens: compare the minimal quadratic target with the general degree-at-most-five
  Abdolmaleki--Kumashiro construction.
