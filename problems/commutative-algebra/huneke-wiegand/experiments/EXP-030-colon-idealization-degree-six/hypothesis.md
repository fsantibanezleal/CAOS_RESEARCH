# EXP-030 hypothesis - cubic-colon idealization and degree-six third strand

Status at declaration: **ACTIVE, NO RESULT CLAIMED**.

## Question

For every integer `p>=4`, determine the complete cubic colon `Q_p:f_p` and decide whether its first
non-Koszul mapping-cone contribution gives all of `beta_(3,6)(C_p)` over every field.

## Falsifiable prediction

Put

```text
H_p={a in E_(p,1):a>=6p},
L_p=E_(p,1) minus H_p=[0,p] union [3p,4p-2],
A_i=X_i,                    0<=i<=p,
B_j=X_(3p+j),               0<=j<=p-2.
```

The experiment predicts:

1. `Q_p:f_p` is generated by the `8p` variables indexed by `H_p` and, on the low variables, by
   all equal-sum `AA` binomials, all equal-sum `AB` binomials, and all `BB` monomials.
2. The low quotient is the canonical idealization
   `k[s,t]^(p) semidirect omega_(k[s,t]^(p))`, under
   `A_i -> s^(p-i)t^i` and `B_j -> epsilon*s^(p-2-j)t^j`, with `epsilon^2=0`.
3. Its Hilbert series is `(1+(2p-2)z+z^2)/(1-z)^2`, and

   ```text
   beta_(1,2)^low=p(2p-3),
   beta_(2,3)^low=8p(p-1)(p-2)/3.
   ```

4. Over the original `10p`-variable polynomial ring,

   ```text
   beta_(2,3)(P_p/(Q_p:f_p))=8p(7p^2-12p+2)/3.
   ```

5. The shifted classes survive primitively and exhaust total-degree-six relative `H_2`, so

   ```text
   beta_(3,6)(C_p)=8p(7p^2-12p+2)/3
   ```

   over every field.

Any extra or missing colon generator, a different Hilbert function, a mismatching relative
`H_2` rank, a nonunit Smith factor, or a characteristic discrepancy refutes the relevant item.

## Method

### Route A: canonical colon reconstruction

Enumerate degree-two low monomials and their equal-offset fibers after the high variables are set
to zero. Compare the exact kernel dimension and a canonical row-space basis with the predicted
`AA`, `AB`, and `BB` generators. Reconstruct Hilbert functions in bounded degrees directly from
normal forms and compare with `2pd` for `d>=1`.

### Route B: exact relative homology

For every relevant offset `b` in total degree six, build the signed relative chain groups with

```text
b-sum(F) in E_(p,6-|F|).
```

Compute `H_2` exactly. The canonical route may use modular ranks only after rational/integral smoke
agreement. Persist the complete offset profile and its total.

### Route C: independent audit and integral gate

Independently rebuild the low quotient from the square-zero parametrization rather than the colon
fiber code. For `p=4`, recompute the relative profile over `GF(2)` and `GF(1000003)`, and obtain
Smith or explicit unit-matching evidence on every nonzero component. The deductive proof must
explain the all-parameter idealization and every surviving relative class; finite agreement is not
the theorem.

## What PASS proves and what FAIL proves

- A colon-only PASS proves the canonical-idealization theorem and its displayed Betti coefficient.
  It does not prove `beta_(3,6)(C_p)`.
- A full PASS through the integral relative gate proves the displayed characteristic-independent
  `beta_(3,6)` formula.
- A finite profile PASS validates implementations only. Without an all-parameter matching proof,
  the degree-six formula remains a conjecture.
- A mismatch refutes the exact prediction at its first failing parameter or offset.
- A budget stop is `INCONCLUSIVE_BUDGET` and supports no mathematical conclusion.

## Invariant-first note

The selected invariant is the low colon quotient itself. Its predicted recognition as a canonical
idealization fixes the Hilbert series and the relevant colon Betti coefficient before any large
chain calculation. The Hilbert numerator of `C_p` cannot isolate `beta_(3,6)`, and the grevlex
initial ideal supplies only an upper bound, so neither replaces the relative-homology gate.
