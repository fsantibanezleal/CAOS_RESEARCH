# EXP-067 hypothesis: simple-gluing transfer of two-generated rigidity

Date: 2026-09-20. Frozen after the source and invariant preflight and before
the producer, independent auditor, or manuscript text is written.

## Question

Let `Gamma` be a numerical semigroup of multiplicity `m`, let `s>0` be a gap,
and suppose the normalized two-generated monomial ideal `(1,t^s)` is rigid.
For an integer `q>=1` with `gcd(q,m)=1`, does the simple gluing

```text
Gamma^(q) = m*N + q*Gamma
```

preserve symmetry and rigidity at the scaled shift `q*s`?

## Falsifiable predictions

### P1: semigroup and Gorenstein transfer

`Gamma^(q)` is a numerical semigroup of multiplicity `m`, with

```text
Ap(Gamma^(q),m) = q*Ap(Gamma,m)
```

after the residue permutation induced by multiplication by `q`. If `Gamma` is
symmetric with Frobenius number `F`, then `Gamma^(q)` is symmetric and

```text
F^(q) = q*(F+m)-m.
```

The scaled shift `q*s` remains a gap.

### P2: exact colon-exponent transfer

Put

```text
E = {a : a,a+s in Gamma},
D = {a : a,a+s,a+2s in Gamma}.
```

For the glued semigroup and shift `q*s`, the complete nonnegative exponent
sets satisfy

```text
E^(q) = m*N + q*E,
D^(q) = m*N + q*D.
```

Consequently `D=E+E` implies
`D^(q)=E^(q)+E^(q)`. The ideal
`(t^(qm),t^(q(m+s)))` in the localized semigroup ring of `Gamma^(q)` is
nonprincipal and rigid.

### P3: two-parameter CAOS consequence

For every `p>=4` and every `q>=1` coprime to `24p`, EXP-009 yields a symmetric
counterexample semigroup

```text
Gamma_(p,q) = 24p*N + q*Gamma_p
```

with multiplicity `24p`, embedding dimension `11p`, and Frobenius number

```text
q*(102p-1)-24p.
```

The rigid ideal is generated by exponents `24pq` and `30pq`. The case `q=1`
is exactly EXP-009; every `q>1` is a new gluing descendant.

### P4: adverse controls

- If `gcd(q,m)>1`, the displayed generators must have nontrivial common gcd,
  so the construction is not a numerical semigroup.
- The nonrigid control `Gamma=<4,5>`, shift `s=1`, must remain nonrigid for
  `q=1,3,5,7`; no implementation may turn the transfer into a false universal
  rigidity claim.
- Perturbing the scaled shift from `q*s` to `q*s+1` is not covered and must not
  be reported as a consequence.

## Method

1. Prove the Apery formula and symmetry transfer directly; cite gluing
   literature for context but do not import the new rigidity conclusion.
2. Prove a simultaneous alignment lemma for the representations of
   `n`, `n+q*s`, and `n+2q*s`. This must establish P2 without finite fitting.
3. Implement a deterministic exact checker using shortest paths for Apery sets
   and the existing complete `D=E+E` checker.
4. Check every `p=4,...,12` and the first eight admissible `q` values, plus the
   adverse controls. An independent implementation must reconstruct the
   semigroup from generators and must not import producer artifacts.
5. Emit JSON artifacts with exact invariants, first-failure fields, and SHA-256
   aggregate hashes.

## What PASS and FAIL prove

A PASS requires the symbolic proof plus both exact implementations. It proves
the general transfer theorem and therefore the two-parameter CAOS corollary;
finite agreement alone cannot confirm either statement.

A FAIL of an exact formula supplies a counterexample to the proposed transfer
and closes this focus. A finite PASS with an incomplete proof is only supporting
evidence and does not authorize a manuscript theorem.

## Premise dependencies

- EXP-009 proves the base CAOS family is symmetric and has `D=E+E` for every
  `p>=4`.
- EXP-001 supplies the nonrigid `<4,5>` adverse control.
- Pham's paper and the Huneke-Iyengar-Wiegand criterion supply the established
  dictionary between `D=E+E` and rigidity; EXP-001 independently validates that
  dictionary in the public example.
- Simple gluing and Gorenstein inheritance are established background, but the
  colon-exponent and rigidity transfer in P2 are hypotheses of this experiment.

## Invariant-first note

The coprimality invariant decides whether the generators can define a numerical
semigroup. Apery thresholds decide membership and symmetry without enumeration.
The representation congruence modulo `m`, not another Kunz-face sweep, is the
candidate one-line invariant for P2.

## Amendment (recorded 2026-09-24): P2 ideal well-posedness

The declared P2 ideal `(t^(qm),t^(q(m+s)))` lies in the glued semigroup ring
only when `m+s in Gamma`, which the declaration does not assume. For
`Gamma=<3,5>` and `s=1`, `m+s=4` is not in `Gamma`, so `t^(4q)` is not in the
ring of `Gamma^(q)`. The corrected statement chooses any `a` with
`a,a+s in Gamma`, uses the integral translate `(t^a,t^(a+s))` of `(1,t^s)`,
and predicts that `(t^(qa),t^(q(a+s)))` is nonprincipal and rigid. For the CAOS
family `a=m`, so the declared and corrected ideals coincide in every tested
case.

The correction was committed together with the results in `db5f4179`, not
before the run, so the record does not show that it preceded the producer. The
question, the exponent-set formulas, and the kill criteria are unchanged. The
verdict evaluates P2 against the corrected statement.

## Addendum 1 (2026-11-15): validation hardening (HWB-095)

Declared before any code of the addendum runs. It changes no prediction of the experiment; it strengthens the evidence
for P2 and P4, which the pre-merge review (verdict, 2026-09-24) found weaker than recorded: the auditor did not recompute
the `E` and `D` transfer sets or rerun the controls, and the five noncoprime controls recorded `gcd(24p, q) > 1` for pairs
chosen with that property without exercising any rejection path.

A new independent script (`audit_v2.py`, which must not import the producer `run.py` or its artifacts, only EXP-009's
base construction through the same loader the existing auditor uses) checks the same 72 cases (`p = 4, ..., 12`, the
first eight admissible `q` for each) and the controls:

- **H1 (E and D recomputed).** For each case, the complete nonnegative exponent sets `E^(q)` and `D^(q)` of the glued
  semigroup `Delta = 24p*N + q*Gamma_p` at the shift `q*s` are computed directly from a membership bitset of `Delta` up to
  its conductor plus `2*q*s` (beyond the conductor every element is in), and compared, element by element, with
  `24p*N + q*E` and `24p*N + q*D` computed from the base sets. Prediction: equal in all 72 cases.
- **H2 (rigidity recomputed).** `D^(q) = E^(q) + E^(q)` is checked directly on the recomputed sets (complete sumset up
  to the same bound). Prediction: holds in all 72 cases.
- **H3 (base premise by full masks).** The base semigroup `Gamma_p` reconstructed by the auditor is compared with
  EXP-009's by the full membership mask up to the conductor, not by summary invariants. Prediction: equal for
  `p = 4, ..., 12`.
- **H4 (embedding dimension by minimality).** The minimal generating set of `Delta` is computed as the nonzero elements
  that are not a sum of two nonzero elements; its size equals `11p`. Prediction: holds in all 72 cases.
- **H5 (the shift is a gap, recorded).** For each case, whether `q*s` is a gap of `Delta` is recorded as a field; a
  non-gap is a recorded failure, not an exception. Prediction: `q*s` is a gap in all 72 cases.
- **H6 (controls rerun).** (a) `Gamma = <4,5>`, `s = 1`: nonrigid for `q = 1, 3, 5, 7`, recomputed by the auditor.
  (b) Real rejection: for the five noncoprime pairs of the original run and five more chosen with `gcd(q, 24p) > 1`, the
  auditor computes the gcd of the generators of `24p*N + q*Gamma_p` and confirms it is greater than 1, so the set is not a
  numerical semigroup (its complement is infinite: the auditor exhibits an integer above any proposed conductor bound that
  is not in it), and confirms that the producer's entry point refuses these inputs (the refusal is observed, not
  assumed). (c) The perturbed shift `q*s + 1` is evaluated and reported only as data, never as a consequence.

Artifacts: `artifacts/audit-v2.json` with every case's fields and a SHA-256 aggregate, and a verdict section
"Addendum 1" with the outcome. A FAIL of H1, H2 or H4 is a counterexample to the recorded result and is reported as
found; the verdict word changes only if a prediction of the experiment fails.

Compute budget: CPU only, exact integers, at most 10 minutes and 2 GiB.
