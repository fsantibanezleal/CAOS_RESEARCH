# EXP-027 proof - the first interior Betti strand

## Theorem

Let `p>=4`, let `P_p=k[X_a : a in G_p]`, and let `C_p=P_p/J_p` be the conductor
special fiber of EXP-021. Give `P_p` the bidegree `deg(X_a)=(1,a)`. Over every field,

```text
beta_(2,4)^(P_p)(C_p)=8p
```

and its offset-graded support is multiplicity-free:

```text
beta_(2,(4,b))=1
```

exactly when

```text
b in S_p
   =[9p,11p-2]
    union [11p,13p-2]
    union {13p}
    union [14p-1,15p-1]
    union [16p+1,17p-2]
    union [17p,18p-1]
    union {19p}
    union [20p-1,21p-1].
```

The adjacent standard-graded entry is

```text
beta_(3,4)^(P_p)(C_p)
  =p(5p-1)(500p^2-440p+47)/2.
```

Equivalently,

```text
S_p={3p+a : a in G_p and a>=6p}.
```

The integral relative first homology in every supported offset is free of rank one. In
particular, none of these statements has a characteristic exception.

## 1. Relative squarefree-divisor chains

EXP-025 gives

```text
C_p=k[x y^a : a in G_p] inside k[x,y]/(y^(24p)).
```

Put

```text
H_p=<(1,a) : a in G_p> subset N^2,
S_p'=k[H_p],
M_p=(t^(n,s) : s>=24p).
```

Then `C_p=S_p'/M_p`. For `u=(j,b)`, set

```text
Delta_u={F subset G_p : u-(|F|,sum(F)) is in H_p},
Gamma_u={F in Delta_u : t^(u-(|F|,sum(F))) is in M_p}.
```

This is the monomial-ideal version of the squarefree divisor construction. It can be derived here
without importing a black box. The offset-`u` part of the Koszul complex on the variables
`X_a` has, in homological degree `r`, one basis vector for every `r`-subset `F` for which

```text
b-sum(F) is in E_(j-r).
```

Every surviving component of `C_p` is one-dimensional. The Koszul differential deletes one
element of `F` with the usual sign, and the term is zero exactly when the target monomial belongs
to `M_p`. Thus this strand is the augmented relative simplicial chain complex of
`(Delta_u,Gamma_u)`, shifted by one. Therefore

```text
beta_(i,u)(C_p)=dim_k H~_(i-1)(Delta_u,Gamma_u;k).                 (1)
```

This direct identification also explains why retaining the offset is essential: summing the
blocks first would hide both the support and the simple homotopy type.

For `j=4`, the chain basis uses only the frozen sets

```text
E_1=G_p,
E_2=[0,2p] union [3p,5p-2] union [6p,24p-1],
E_3=[0,24p-1] minus {6p-1},
E_4=[0,24p-1].
```

## 2. A unit matching gives the upper bound

Fix an offset `b`. Order `G_p` increasingly. On the relative chain basis in (1), scan vertices in
that order. At vertex `v`, pair every still-unmatched cell `F` not containing `v` with
`F union {v}` whenever both are surviving relative cells; within one scan use increasing
dimension and lexicographic order.

Each pair is a unit entry of the signed boundary matrix. The pivot order strictly increases along
every reversed matched arrow, so the matching is acyclic. It is therefore a sequence of integral
elementary chain cancellations, not a field-dependent rank argument.

Substitution of the four displayed `E_i` sets gives the following complete list of critical cells
in relative dimensions zero and one. Write `b=6p+r` in the middle row.

| offset | critical relative 0-cells | critical relative 1-cells | relevant Morse boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b in S_p` | `{0}` | `{p,b-3p}` | zero in degree one or killed from above |
| `6p<=b<=7p-3` | `{0}`, `{r+2}` | `{p,3p+r}` | a unit times `{r+2}-{0}` |
| every other `b` | immaterial | none | none |

Here is the interval check behind the table. A size-`r` cell `F` survives precisely when

```text
b-sum(F) in E_(4-r),       r=1,2,3.
```

At each pivot `v`, the only obstruction to toggling `v` is one of the three gaps

```text
[2p+1,3p-1], [5p-1,6p-1], or {6p-1}
```

in `E_2` or `E_3`, together with a gap of `G_p`. Scanning the eleven blocks of `G_p` leaves
`{p,a}` exactly for `b=3p+a` with `a>=6p`; it also leaves the transient cells in the second row.
In that row, the unique alternating path from the critical edge to each critical vertex has
signed difference `+1`, giving the displayed unit boundary. All other candidate edges toggle at
their first available vertex. No count or characteristic enters this reduction.

Consequently, for every field,

```text
beta_(2,(4,b)) <= 1  if b in S_p,
beta_(2,(4,b)) = 0   if b is not in S_p.                         (2)
```

The transient interval contributes no homology because its sole critical edge has nonzero unit
boundary. The matching does not need to classify the numerous critical relative 2-cells in order
to prove (2).

## 3. The quadratic colon gives the matching lower bound

By EXP-023,

```text
J_p=(Q_p,f_p),
f_p=X_0^2X_(3p)-X_p^3,
```

where `Q_p` is generated by all minimal quadratic relations. Two degree-four monomials lie in the
same `Q_p`-fiber component when their factorizations are connected by replacements of two
variables with two variables having the same offset sum.

Define the set of centered offsets

```text
V_p={v in G_p : v-p and v+p are in G_p}
   =[7p,8p-2] union [8p,9p-2] union {9p,10p}.                    (3)
```

For every `a in G_p` with `a>=6p`, the factorizations

```text
(0,0,3p,a) and (p,p,p,a)
```

are connected by one of three explicit paths. Consecutive rows differ by a quadratic relation.

If `a+p,a+2p` belong to `G_p`, use

```text
(0,0,3p,a)
~ (0,0,p,a+2p)
~ (0,p,p,a+p)
~ (p,p,p,a).
```

For the sole bridge `a=7p-1`, use

```text
(0,0,3p,7p-1)
~ (0,1,3p,7p-2)
~ (0,1,p,9p-2)
~ (1,p,p,8p-2)
~ (p,p,p,7p-1).
```

In the remaining cases, write `a=u+v` with `u in G_p` and `v in V_p`, and use

```text
(0,0,3p,a)
~ (0,3p,u,v)
~ (p,3p,u,v-p)
~ (p,p,u,v+p)
~ (p,p,p,a).
```

If the first two rows coincide, omit the repeated row. Equations (3) and the eleven blocks of
`G_p` show directly that these cases cover every high offset. The symbolic certificate proves the
negation of this coverage formula UNSAT for arbitrary integer `p>=4`.

For low offsets, the two factorizations cannot be connected. Indeed, if `a<=p`, their total is at
most `4p`; if `3p<=a<=4p-2`, it is at most `7p-2`. Below `7p`, color a generator odd exactly when
it lies in the middle block `[3p,4p-2]`. Every equality of two pair sums below `7p` preserves the
parity of the number of middle-block variables: the only cross-block equality can replace two
middle variables by one low and one high variable, changing that number by two. The start and end
factorizations have opposite parity in both low cases. The fifth symbolic query independently
certifies that no parity-changing pair equality exists.

It follows that

```text
(Q_p:f_p)_1=span_k{X_a : a in G_p and a>=6p},                   (4)
```

and this vector space has dimension `8p`.

Now use the homogeneous exact sequence

```text
0 -> P_p/(Q_p:f_p)(-3) --f_p--> P_p/Q_p -> C_p -> 0.           (5)
```

In the mapping cone of minimal resolutions, every linear generator in (4) supplies a
homological-degree-two summand shifted by four. It cannot cancel: its maps to the shift-three
copy of `P_p` by a linear entry and to the shift-two quadratic-generator module by degree-two
entries. All entries lie in the homogeneous maximal ideal. Thus (5) gives one nonzero
`beta_(2,(4,3p+a))` for every high `a`.

This lower bound and (2) prove the multiplicity-free support theorem and

```text
beta_(2,4)=|S_p|=8p.                                           (6)
```

The matching is integral and each mapping-cone class is primitive. Hence each supported integral
relative homology group is `Z`, not `Z` plus hidden torsion. This proves the characteristic-free
statement and, as a byproduct, `beta_(2,4)(P_p/Q_p)=0`: the quadratic ideal is linearly presented
through this first nonlinear position.

## 4. The adjacent entry

EXP-024 gives the Hilbert numerator

```text
(1-z)^(10p-1)(1+(10p-1)z+12pz^2+(2p-1)z^3+z^4).
```

There are no minimal degree-four equations by EXP-023. Since `C_p` has no linear equations, the
minimal shift in homological degree `i` is at least `i+1`; therefore only `beta_(2,4)` and
`beta_(3,4)` contribute to the coefficient of `z^4`. Direct expansion gives

```text
[z^4] = -p(2500p^3-2700p^2+675p-63)/2
      = beta_(2,4)-beta_(3,4).
```

Substituting (6) yields

```text
beta_(3,4)=p(5p-1)(500p^2-440p+47)/2.
```

The numerator is even: if `p` is even this is immediate, and if `p` is odd then `5p-1` is even.

## Scope boundary

EXP-027 determines the first unknown interior standard-graded strand and its complete offset
support. It does not determine the remaining interior Betti table, does not claim a pure or
closed-form full resolution, and does not alter the status of the original Huneke--Wiegand
conjecture beyond the already published counterexample family.
