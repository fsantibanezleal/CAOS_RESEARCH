conductor special fiber, and write

```text
C_p=P_p/J_p,
P_p=k[X_a : a in G_p].
```

```text
C_p isomorphic to k[x y^a : a in G_p] inside k[x,y]/(y^q).
```

The dehomogenized algebra, complete primary decomposition, and sharp nilpotence are

```text
C_p/(X_0-1) isomorphic to k[y]/(y^q),
sqrt(J_p)=L_p=(X_a:a>0),
J_p is L_p-primary,
nilindex(nil(C_p))=q.
```

In particular, `Proj(C_p)` is a saturated length-`q` curvilinear fat point supported at the
`X_0` coordinate point and has tangent dimension one. It is locally Gorenstein, while `C_p` is
not level and not Gorenstein and has Cohen--Macaulay type `10p+1`.

For `B_p=C_p/(X_0-1)`, the differential fingerprint over any field is

```text
Omega^1_(B_p/k) isomorphic to (B_p/(q y^(q-1)))dy,
exterior^m Omega^1_(B_p/k)=0 for m>=2.
```

Thus its dimension is `q-1` when `char(k)` does not divide `q` and `q` when it does. The
cotangent and tangent dimensions remain one in every characteristic.

## Trust and scope boundary

parameters. The symbolic proof in `proof.md` is load-bearing and imports only hash-frozen
EXP-021/023/024 premises. EXP-023's all-parameter value-congruence theorem retains its disclosed
solver/encoding boundary. No claim is made for arbitrary conductor ideals, arbitrary fiber cones,
or arbitrary Huneke--Wiegand counterexamples.
