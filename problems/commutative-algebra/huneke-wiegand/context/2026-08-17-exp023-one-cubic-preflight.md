# EXP-023 preflight - corrected one-cubic defining presentation

Date: 2026-08-17.

## Redirect from EXP-022

EXP-022 proves that the conductor special-fiber defining ideal `J_p` has exactly
`50p^2-17p` minimal quadrics but cannot be generated by them: the value relation

```text
F_p=X_0^2X_(3p)-X_p^3
```

is isolated under quadratic moves for every `p>=4`. Complete exact runs at `p=4,5,6` find that
this is the only cubic and that no minimal equations occur in degrees four or five. That finite
pattern is the corrected candidate, not an EXP-022 conclusion.

## Corrected target

Let `K_p` be the complete quadratic kernel. Test and prove

```text
J_p=(K_p,F_p),
beta_(1,2)=50p^2-17p,
beta_(1,3)=1,
beta_(1,j)=0 for j>=4.
```

This would give relation type three and exactly `50p^2-17p+1` minimal defining equations. It
would also prove that `C_p` is not Koszul, since a standard graded Koszul algebra has a quadratic
defining ideal.

## Source boundary

Abdolmaleki--Kumashiro Theorem 2.8 remains the completeness backstop: because `C_p` is
Cohen--Macaulay and `red_Q(T_p)=4`, no minimal equation occurs above degree five. The source does
not imply the one-cubic profile. EXP-023 must close degrees three, four, and five uniformly.

## Scalable congruence-state route

For degree `d>=3`, quotienting by all variable multiples of the complete degree-`d-1` kernel can
be done without enumerating degree-`d` monomials. A state is a pair

```text
(a,b),  a in E_1, b in E_(d-1), total t=a+b,
```

representing `X_a` times the unique degree-`d-1` class of offset `b`. For fixed `t`, states
`(a,t-a)` and `(c,t-c)` are connected whenever

```text
r=t-a-c belongs to E_(d-2).
```

This is the common degree-`d` monomial with remaining offset `r`. A state is connected to zero if
the same decomposition has one invalid degree-`d-1` remainder. Thus the number of nonzero state
components minus `|E_d|` is exactly `beta_(1,d)`. This route has only `O(p^2)` states and is
independent of EXP-022's symmetric-monomial enumeration.

## Symbolic proof obligation

The degree-three statement reduces to a family of finite interval graphs indexed by total offset
`t`. Prove that:

1. every zero total is connected to the zero node;
2. every nonzero total has one component, except `t=3p` before adjoining `F_p`;
3. at `t=3p` the only two components are represented by `X_0^2X_(3p)` and `X_p^3`.

For degrees four and five, repeat the state-graph argument using the full preceding kernel and
prove one component for every nonzero total and zero connectivity otherwise. Interval expansion,
not finite sampling, is load-bearing.

## Compute and stop conditions

- First reproduce `p=4,5,6` by the state route and reject any disagreement with EXP-022.
- Then run a bounded campaign selected by scaling behavior. CPU budget: five minutes; exact
  integer/set arithmetic; checkpoint summaries by parameter.
- Independently rehash all campaign rows and rebuild selected parameters with a separately
  encoded route. Audit budget: two minutes.
- Any extra component is a refutation. Preserve its degree, total, and state witnesses and stop
  the affected theorem claim.
- A finite pass remains `[MV]`; `CONFIRMED` requires the uniform interval-graph proof and the
  source-backed degree-five completeness step.

## Novelty and publication boundary

The primary-source pass found the general construction but no presentation for this explicit
family. A proved uniform minimal presentation, relation type, and non-Koszul consequence would be
material and would trigger manuscript deliberation. Computational evidence or a partial lower
bound does not trigger Zenodo.

## Exploration moment

The failed quadratic conjecture identifies the obstruction as an additive circuit rather than a
large elimination artifact: `0+0+3p=p+p+p`. The corrected path treats minimal equations as
connected components of factorization graphs. This separates one primitive circuit from the
quadratic sea and gives a plausible route to a complete presentation without computing a full
Groebner basis in `10p` variables.
