# EXP-025 curvilinear and primary-structure preflight

Date: 2026-08-18. Status: source-complete declaration preflight.

## Question and redirection

After EXP-024, the open quadratic/Groebner-basis route is still potentially expensive and does
not yet have a uniform candidate. A different invariant-first view of the same conductor special
fiber is much sharper. EXP-021 proves that `C_p` is free of rank `q=24p` over `k[X_0]`, while
EXP-023 proves that a monomial class is determined by its total offset and vanishes exactly when
that offset is absent. Since the degree-one offset set contains `0` and `1`, these two results
suggest that the entire nonreduced projective geometry is one-variable and may admit a complete
primary decomposition without computing a Groebner basis.

The selected question is therefore:

> Is `C_p` the standard-graded subalgebra generated by `x y^a` inside
> `k[x,y]/(y^(24p))`, and does this force `J_p` to be primary with a curvilinear
> length-`24p` projective scheme?

This route ranks above HWB-029 for the present round because it has a short falsifiable
parametrization, exact finite invariants, and a deductive bridge from already frozen theorems.
HWB-029 remains open rather than being silently relabeled.

## Pre-declaration sanity check

offset bases at `p=4,5,17,73,151,300`. In every row:

- the degree-one basis has size `10p` and contains offsets `0,1,2`;

This check only supports declaration. It does not prove or confirm EXP-025.

## Candidate theorem and proof route

Put `q=24p`, let `G=E_1`, and assign degree one to every `x y^a`. The candidate is

```text
C_p isomorphic to k[x y^a : a in G] inside k[x,y]/(y^q),
X_a maps to x y^a.
```

EXP-023's value-congruence theorem would make the degree-`n` image basis exactly `E_n`. Since
`0,1 in G`, the homogeneous equalities

```text
X_0^(a-1) X_a = X_1^a
```

should hold for every positive `a in G`. Setting `X_0=1` would then give a surjection
`k[y]/(y^q) -> C_p/(X_0-1)`. EXP-021 gives both sides dimension `q`, so the map should be an
isomorphism. The remaining primary and geometric assertions would follow from this model plus
one-dimensional Cohen--Macaulayness.
