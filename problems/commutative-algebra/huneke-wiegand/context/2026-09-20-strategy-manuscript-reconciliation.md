# Strategy and manuscript reconciliation after EXP-066

Date: 2026-09-20.

## Purpose of this review

The program began after the public counterexample was known. Its initial
extension surface was minimality, classification, infinite families, and
endomorphism structure. Those objectives were substantially closed by
EXP-005, EXP-007, EXP-009, and EXP-011. Later work produced valid family-level
commutative-algebra and homological results, but the program did not maintain a
single user-visible boundary between the original purpose and the derived
questions.

This review does not retract the validated mathematics. It corrects research
governance: each focus now has a target, value statement, theorem-sized success
gate, stop conditions, novelty status, and manuscript route.

## Value assessment

The direct minimality, uniqueness, and infinite-family results remain the most
relevant CAOS extensions because they answer natural questions immediately
created by the public example. The conductor and curvilinear results form
coherent specialized algebraic stories. The Betti and carrier programs contain
uniform theorems, but their relevance depends on completing a structural object
rather than accumulating additional cells or projections.

EXP-066 is useful as a constructive formula, but it is not by itself a reason
for an open-ended endpoint campaign. The carrier path now has a strict gate: a
canonical comparison and a complete kernel, quotient, or reusable matching
theorem. Finite carrier tables do not meet that gate.

## Reprioritization

The new first-priority research focus is a mechanism-classification question in
Kunz coordinates: determine whether the explicit CAOS family lies in a
positive-dimensional face on which the two-generated rigidity identity admits
necessary and sufficient linear or sumset conditions. This focus reconnects
the work to classification of counterexamples rather than another invariant of
one presentation.

Subsequent same-day preflight corrected this wording. The multiplicity is
`24p`, so the family lies in different Kunz cones rather than one face. Small
face diagnostics exposed the stronger simple-gluing invariant, and the active
route is now `HW-F7` / EXP-067. See
`2026-09-20-kunz-gluing-preflight.md`. The four items below are retained as the
historical admission gate that caused the correction before computation.

No experiment is declared by this review. Before computation, the focus needs:

1. a source-complete Kunz-polyhedron and numerical-semigroup rigidity review;
2. a precise face or block model independent of the existing parameter formula;
3. one invariant-first test on the smallest parameters;
4. a frozen theorem target and explicit resource cap.

If any of those gates fails, the focus returns to the research record without a
manuscript or compute campaign.

## Manuscript decision

All major internally proved result blocks have a manuscript home. The problem
is coherence, not absence. The 53-page consolidated minimality paper now holds
three different research stories. It remains an immutable public source record,
but future submission work is split into:

- counterexample classification and families;
- conductor and tangent/special-fiber algebra;
- Betti and syzygy anatomy.

The curvilinear and integral-torsion companions are already coherent. The
integral companion receives no further version unless the canonical comparison
or complete-quotient gate is met.

## External novelty boundary

The recorded primary-source searches did not locate exact versions of the CAOS
extension theorems. This is evidence of a plausible novelty surface, not proof
of worldwide priority. Journal submission or strong public novelty language
requires an updated database search and independent specialist review for each
focused paper.
