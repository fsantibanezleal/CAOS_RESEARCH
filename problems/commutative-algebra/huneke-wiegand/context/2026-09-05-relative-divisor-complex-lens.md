# Relative divisor-complex lens, 2026-09-05

Primary source: Winfried Bruns and Jurgen Herzog, *Semigroup rings and simplicial
complexes*, Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 122 (1997), 185-208,
[author-hosted published paper](https://www.home.uni-osnabrueck.de/wbruns/brunsw/pdf-article/SimpSemi.published.pdf).
Read the full Proposition 1.1 and its proof on printed pages 186-187 from
rendered pages, because the scanned PDF has no extractable text.

For a positive affine semigroup ring modulo a monomial ideal, Proposition 1.1
identifies a multigraded Koszul strand with a shifted relative simplicial
chain complex, using squarefree divisors and the faces killed by the ideal.
The isomorphism is obtained directly from the oriented monomial bases.

## Applicability boundary and proposed use

The CAOS Artinian presentation is not automatically a simplicial complex
merely because its matrix entries are 0 and plus/minus 1. Before importing
the proposition, identify the relevant semigroup quotient, its killed ideal,
and the complete graded chain identification. No such identification is
claimed in this note. The integral signed basis identification must also be
proved when the target is integral torsion rather than field dimensions.

An immediately usable internal analogue is the low-exterior complement:
fixed high sets turn the original missing-index equations into insertion
incidence. EXP-059 tests a complete integer potential parametrization in one
fixed-high sector, without needing an unproved global topological model.
Its predicted seven-row connecting map could then be compared with a
relative simplicial or signed-incidence presentation through explicit maps.

This is an alternative proof language, not a new numerical rank prediction
or a theorem that the CAOS family is a projective-plane complex. EXP-036's
failed six-variable recognition and EXP-058's local obstruction remain intact.
