# Realizability of integral points on pure rays (Boij-Soderberg theory)

The Betti table of a finite length graded module over the polynomial ring k[x,y,z] records how many generators, relations
and higher syzygies it has in each degree. Boij-Soderberg theory shows that every such table is a positive combination of
pure tables, one for each degree sequence (0,d1,d2,d3), and that each pure table is unique up to a rational multiple.
Which integer multiples are tables of actual modules is open. Erman and Sam asked whether every multiple is realized over
k[x,y,z] or over the enveloping algebra of the Heisenberg Lie algebra; in May 2026 the Rethlas system answered no with the
sequence (0,6,20,21). Smaller counterexamples, with largest degree 10, follow from published obstructions.
