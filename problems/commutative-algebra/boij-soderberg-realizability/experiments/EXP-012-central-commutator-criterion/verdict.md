## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The seed of family R is 20261120 plus the index of the pair counted from 0 (the hypothesis does not say from which
  number the index counts).
- In family R the ranks are computed until the first degree whose dimension differs from h_e.
- In G1 the pair <x^4, y^4> over GF(5) has no Betti table: the module is not of finite length within the degree bound,
  which is the known answer "not pure" in a stronger form.

## How could this be wrong?

- The criterion is decided by a Groebner basis computed by one library; the review decided it by another route on its
  180 pairs, and the two agree with purity in every pair.
- The experiment checks cases; the statement for every e = 0 or 1 modulo p rests on the proof.
