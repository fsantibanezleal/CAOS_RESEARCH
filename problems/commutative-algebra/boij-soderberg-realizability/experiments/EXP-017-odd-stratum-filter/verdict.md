# EXP-017 verdict: CONFIRMED. The filter of two congruences finds pure pairs (x^e + alpha(xy, z) y, y^e + beta(xy, z) x) over GF(p) at (9, 11), (9, 13), (11, 7) and (13, 5); in the eight cases every member that passes the filter has the pure Hilbert function and every table computed is pure

not settled. Exact arithmetic: the filter with python-flint, the rank routine of EXP-010 (imported) and `uhlib`
(first instrument), Singular 4.3.2 with Plural (second instrument). Numbers: `results-summary.md` and

## What is established, and by what

1. The point (1, 2, 2, 1) of (0, 11, 33, 44) is realized over U over GF(7), for example by
   f = x^11 + 2 x^5 y^6 - x^4 y^5 z + 2 x^3 y^4 z^2 + 3 x^2 y^3 z^3 - 3 x y^2 z^4 + 2 y z^5,
   g = y^11 - x^6 y^5 - x^5 y^4 z + 2 x^3 y^2 z^3 + 2 x^2 y z^4 - 2 x z^5: length 1331, pure table. The point of
   (0, 13, 39, 52) is realized over GF(5), for example by
   f = x^13 - 2 x^6 y^7 + x^5 y^6 z - 2 x^3 y^4 z^3 - x^2 y^3 z^4 + 2 x y^2 z^5 + 2 y z^6,
   g = y^13 + x^7 y^6 + 2 x^6 y^5 z + x^5 y^4 z^2 + x^4 y^3 z^3 + 2 x^2 y z^5 + 2 x z^6: length 2197, pure table.
   [MV, two instruments, and rebuilt by the coordinating session] Neither pair (e, p) was reached by a theorem (p
   divides neither e nor e - 1, and no pull-back applies) or by an earlier search.
2. The point of (0, 9, 27, 36) is realized over GF(11) and over GF(13) by pairs of this stratum as well, for example
   f = x^9 + 4 x^4 y^5 - 2 x^3 y^4 z - 2 x^2 y^3 z^2 + 4 x y^2 z^3,
   g = y^9 - x^5 y^4 + 4 x^4 y^3 z - 3 x^3 y^2 z^2 + 4 x^2 y z^3 + 3 x z^4 over GF(11). [MV, as above] EXP-015 settled
   these two pairs in other strata; EXP-014 had found no pure member of this stratum there among 300,000.
3. The filter. On everything computed here and before, a member of the stratum is pure exactly when it passes
   (F0) to (F3): in this experiment the 1,699 members that pass have the pure Hilbert function and the 75 tabled are
   pure; among the 130 members of the stratum stored by EXP-010 and EXP-014 with a computed table, the 59 pure ones
   pass and the 71 others do not. This is an observation; the statement is not proved (the decomposition behind
   it is checked by machine and written as working notes only).
4. The density of the members that pass is of the order p^(-(e-3)), as the count of e - 3 conditions on e
   parameters suggests: between 0.25 and 0.62 times that number in the six cases of families K and Q.
   A random search in U of the same size would not have found them: at (9, 13) one member in 7.8 million passes.

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The run was made in two parts. The implementer ran the gates and families K and Q and started family X, then reached
  the limit of its turns; its report is PARTIAL and is recorded as such in the coordination repository. The
  coordinating session ran family X, the second instrument and the table with the same runner, 12 worker processes.
- The case (13, 5) first stopped on an overflow error of the routine of EXP-010 (its tensor of structure constants at
  e = 13, n = 49; the error EXP-015 met). The coordinating session added to `exp017_routine.py` the replacement
  EXP-015 uses (each coefficient reduced modulo p before it is stored; the same tensor modulo p, compared with the
  original in three cases; the 16 tests pass) and ran the case again from its start. The file of EXP-010 was not
  edited, and no earlier case is affected: an entry that does not fit raises an error instead of wrapping.
- The Hilbert function is tested only on the members that pass the whole filter; nothing is computed in U for the
  members that fail it.
- The first congruence is tested on blocks with numpy before the exact test (as the brief allowed); the block test and
  the exact test agree on every member that reached the exact test (the count `inconsistent` is 0 in every case), and
  a unit test compares them on 20,000 members at two pairs.
- Every case stopped once it had five pure members: the control (5, 7) used its whole budget of 200,000, (7, 5) two
  thirds of its budget, and the six other cases 5 to 13 percent. Time: 442 seconds accounted by the runner of the
  10,800 allowed (the run of (13, 5) that stopped on the error is not in that count).

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on 24 pure members; four controls with known answers; a fixed member.
- Re-check by the coordinating session, without the routine of this experiment (script and log under the data root):
  the first two pure members of every case rebuilt from the stored coefficient vectors with a separate parser; (alpha,
  beta) recovered from the pair with the writer's own conversion and compared with the stored ones; the writer's own
  implementation of the filter; the Betti table with the pure Python module construction of `uhlib`: 16 members in the
  eight cases; the recovered (alpha, beta) equal the stored ones, the writer's filter passes each with the scalar mu
  not zero, and all are pure, of lengths 125, 343, 729, 1331 and 2197. 0 findings.
- The filter was fixed before the run from settled pairs only, and the two pairs of the question were not computed
  before the hypothesis was frozen.

## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation repeated by a second system and a second construction; that part does not
  depend on the filter.
- The statement "pure exactly when the filter passes" is supported in one direction by 75 tables and 1,699 Hilbert
  functions, and in the other only by the 71 stored members with another table, all of which had the pure Hilbert
  function: no member that fails the filter was tabled here. It is an observation to be proved or refuted.
- The ratios of P1 at (11, 7) and (13, 5) rest on 6 and 5 members.
