## What is established, and by what

1. The table over S (220 rays, d3 <= 12). Primitive point realized on 193 rays, excluded on 12, open on 15.
   - Realized in characteristic 0 on 147 rays: 78 by a formula construction (52 of them over every field or every
     infinite field), the others by an explicit module over QQ (a search hit rechecked over QQ, or its dual by Ext^3).
     Realized over GF(32003) only, so far, on 46 rays, all with d3 >= 10.
   - Excluded on 12 rays by O1 to O6, and on each of them twice the primitive point is realized, so c_d = 2:
     (0,1,3,4), (0,1,5,6), (0,1,7,8), (0,1,9,10), (0,1,11,12), (0,2,6,8), (0,2,10,12), (0,3,5,8), (0,3,9,12), (0,5,7,12)
     (ten rays that pass parity) and (0,4,9,10), (0,1,6,10) (parity fails).
   - Open on 15 rays, all with d3 >= 10: (0,3,6,10), (0,3,6,11), (0,3,7,10), (0,3,7,11), (0,3,7,12), (0,3,8,11),
     (0,3,8,12), (0,4,7,10), (0,4,7,11), (0,4,7,12), (0,4,8,11), (0,4,9,12), (0,5,8,11), (0,5,8,12), (0,5,9,12). A miss of a
     generic module proves nothing; these points are open, not excluded.
   - c_d is determined on 205 rays: 1 on 193 and 2 on 12.
2. The rays with d3 <= 9 (84 rays). In characteristic 0 the primitive point is realized over S on 79 of them, by a
   formula or by an explicit module over QQ whose Betti table was computed exactly. The other 5, (0,1,3,4), (0,1,5,6),
   (0,1,7,8), (0,2,6,8) and (0,3,5,8), are excluded over S by O1 to O6, pass parity, and are realized over U(H) in
   characteristic 0 (EXP-002: the trivial module, three Kostant modules and U/U(x^2, y^2)). So over a field of
   characteristic 0 every primitive point with d3 <= 9 is the Betti table of a finite length module over k[x,y,z] or
   over U(H): Question 6.1 of Erman and Sam has no counterexample with d3 <= 9 in characteristic 0. [MV for the modules,
   with the cited theorems for the formulas and the obstructions.]
3. Two counterexamples with d3 = 10, over every field. On (0,4,9,10) and (0,1,6,10) (dual to each other) the primitive
   point is excluded over S (a cyclic module with three relations forces a regular sequence, O5; on the dual, the
   regularity bound and the equality case of the codimension bound, O3 and O2), and over U(H) no multiple of it is
   realizable because p(-1) is not zero. Both arguments are free of the characteristic. The published negative answer
   (Jiang, Li, Sun, Wang, Xiao and Yu, arXiv:2605.25259, Theorem 3.7) uses (0,6,20,21), with d3 = 21. With item 2, in
   characteristic 0 the least d3 of a counterexample to Question 6.1 is 10. [The two exclusions are derived from
   published obstructions; the statement is to be read by a reviewer before it is claimed (BSB-015, third item).]
4. Characteristic. In characteristic 2 the point (1,2,2,1) on (0,2,6,8) is a counterexample with d3 = 8 (EXP-003); item 2
   is a statement in characteristic 0 only. Stage 1e gives flags, not claims: over GF(2), GF(3) and GF(5) a generic
   module gives the pure table for at least one of 5 seeds on 98, 126 and 130 of the 130 generators.

## Adversarial validation

Two independent implementations of the census (Python and Macaulay2) on all 220 rays; known answers first (G3); every
hit recomputed from its saved presentation in a fresh process and in a second system; the closure rules (scaling,
duality, direct sums) applied by the script and checked for conflicts with the obstructions (none; G4); the QQ recheck
as a test that could fail, and did on two hits, which are reported as such.

## How could this be wrong?

- The realizations over QQ rest on Macaulay2's resolutions over QQ (one system; the GF(32003) originals are also
  checked in Singular). An error in `res` over QQ common to all 64 runs would affect item 2 on the 42 rays with d3 <= 9
  that are realized in characteristic 0 only through an explicit module (37 have a formula construction).
- The formula constructions and O1 to O6 are cited theorems, used as read in the dossier and the literature pass; an
  error in a transcribed hypothesis (the characteristic 0 requirement of the Eisenbud-Floystad-Weyman constructions,
  the infinite field of Soderberg's proposition) would move a ray between columns. The census tests cover the formulas
  on the published examples.
- The duality closure uses Ext^3(-, S) on a finite length module with a pure resolution [D in the dossier].
- Item 3 excludes the primitive points only; nothing here says which larger multiples are realizable over U(H) on
  parity-failing rays (none is, by the same parity argument) or over S beyond what the table records.
- The 15 open rays and the 46 rays realized only over GF(32003) are not statements about characteristic 0.
