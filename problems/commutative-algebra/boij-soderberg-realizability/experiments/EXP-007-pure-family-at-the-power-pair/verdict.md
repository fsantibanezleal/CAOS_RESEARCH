## What is established, and by what

1. In characteristic 0, for e = 4, 5, 6, 7, the determinantal locus D_e has tangent space of dimension exactly 4e - 4
   at W_0. With the bound of the reviewed note (every component of D_e through a pure point has dimension at least
   4e - 4), D_e is smooth of dimension 4e - 4 at W_0, so exactly one component of the locus of pure pairs passes
   through W_0 and its dimension is 4e - 4. [MV for the tangent dimension, three independent computations: the
   review, this experiment, the coordinating session; the conclusion uses the note's statement (a) to (c) and the fact
   that a variety whose tangent space at a point has the dimension of a lower bound for its local dimension is
   smooth there]
2. The exactness depends on the characteristic: at e = 6 over GF(13) and at e = 7 over GF(17), both at least 2e, the
   tangent space is larger by 2 (22 and 26). What the locus looks like there is not determined here. [MV]
3. Explicit pure cyclic modules over the rationals that are not in the published family, each with the Betti table
   (1, 2, 2, 1): U/U(x^4 + x y^3, y^4); U/U(x^4 + 31 x^2 y^2 - 8 xyz, y^4); U/U(x^4 + 59 x^2 y^2 - 4 z^2, y^4);
   U/U(x^4 + 2 x y^3 - 3 y^2 z, y^4 + 5 x^3 y + 7 x^2 z); U/U(x^5 + 2 x y^4 - 3 y^3 z, y^5 + 5 x^4 y + 7 x^3 z). None is
   U/U(l_1^e, l_2^e) for linear forms l_1, l_2 over any extension field. [MV, two instruments, and a third computation
   by the coordinating session]
4. The four-parameter linear family L_e(a, b, c, d) = <x^e + a x y^(e-1) + b y^(e-2) z, y^e + c x^(e-1) y + d x^(e-2) z>
   lies in D_e on every member tested (625 over the rationals and 200 over GF(32003), for e = 4 and e = 5), and its
   random members over GF(32003) are all pure. The review derived the inclusion from the weights of the diagonal torus;
   the experiment tests it and does not prove it. [MV for the members tested; the inclusion for all parameters is the
   review's derivation, D]
5. The section <x^4 + alpha x^2 y^2 + beta xyz + gamma z^2, y^4> meets D_4 as the review's equation says on every
   point tested. [MV on 735 points]
6. The spaces whose forms have a common factor of degree h_e(3e) in k[x, y] lie in D_e (e = 4, 5, 6) and at e = 4 have
   infinite length: D_e has a component that contains no pure point, as the reviewed note states. [MV on 60 spaces; D
   in the note]

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The limit of 20 minutes per case was a deadline checked inside the loops; no case came near it (the whole run took
  81 seconds).
- T6 used 20 spaces at e = 5 and at e = 6; the hypothesis gives the number for e = 4 only.
- The tangent space at e = 7 over GF(13) was not computed: 13 is below 2e and the hypothesis names no value there.
- A weakness of the hypothesis itself, not of the run: the grid of T5 contains one point of the plane.

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on every Betti table of T3; the tangent routine tested on generic determinantal varieties with
  known dimension; the matrix B tested against a direct product.
- A separate implementation by the coordinating session, written before the results of this experiment were read and
  sharing none of its routine (exact ranks with python-flint over the rationals, its own elimination modulo a prime,
  the product of `uhlib`): the tangent dimensions of T1 and T2 in all fifteen cases, the ranks, Betti tables and orbit
  dimensions of the nine spaces of T3, 61 points of the section of T5 and 25 members of each linear family. Every
  value agrees with this experiment and with the review.

## How could this be wrong?

- The three computations of the tangent space use the same definition (the tangent space of the locus of rank at
  most r at a matrix of rank exactly r). The gate G2 checks the routine on a case with a known answer; an error in the
  definition itself would escape. It is the standard description of the tangent space of a determinantal variety at a
  point of the top stratum.
- The tangent space of D_e is computed for the reduced or scheme structure given by the minors; smoothness of the
  variety at W_0 follows because the tangent dimension equals the lower bound of the local dimension.
- Items 4 and 5 are tests on finite sets of members; the statements for all parameters rest on the review's weight
  argument, which has not been written as a proof in the repository.
- Nothing here concerns odd characteristic below 2e, where W_0 is not pure.
