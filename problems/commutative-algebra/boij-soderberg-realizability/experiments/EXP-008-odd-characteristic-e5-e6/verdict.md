## What is established, and by what

1. e = 5 in characteristics 5 and 7. Over GF(5) the pair
   f = y z^2 + 2 y^3 z + 2 y^5 - 2 x y^2 z - x y^4 + 2 x^2 yz + 2 x^2 y^3 + x^3 z + x^3 y^2 - 2 x^4 y,
   g = -2 y z^2 + y^3 z + y^5 - 2 x y^2 z + x y^4 - x^2 y^3 + 2 x^3 z + x^3 y^2 + x^4 y + x^5
   (PBW monomials x^i y^j z^k) gives a module U/(Uf + Ug) of length 125 with the pure Betti table (1, 2, 2, 1) on
   (0, 5, 15, 20); over GF(7) so does the pair of seed 20261985 printed in `results-summary.md`. With the pair over
   GF(3) of EXP-006, Theorem A (characteristic 0 or at least 10) and Theorem C (characteristic 2): the point (1,2,2,1)
   of (0, 5, 15, 20) is realized over U exactly in the characteristics other than 2. So the statement holds for
   e = 2, 3, 4 and 5. [MV, two instruments, and rebuilt by the coordinating session; the theorems are those of the
   manuscript]
2. e = 6 in characteristic 3. Over GF(3) the pair
   f = z^3 + y^4 z + y^6 - x y z^2 + x y^3 z - x y^5 + x^2 z^2 - x^2 y^4 - x^4 z + x^4 y^2 - x^6,
   g = -z^3 - y^6 - x y z^2 - x y^3 z - x y^5 - x^2 y^2 z + x^2 y^4 - x^3 yz + x^3 y^3
   gives a module of length 216 with the pure table on (0, 6, 18, 24). So that point is realized in characteristic 3
   (and in characteristic 0 or at least 12 by Theorem A, never in characteristic 2); characteristics 5, 7 and 11 stay
   open. [MV, as above]
3. The shares of pure spaces among random ones are 0.31 q^(-4) over GF(5), 0.62 q^(-4) over GF(7) and 0.91 q^(-8) over
   GF(3): the order of magnitude that a pure locus of dimension 4e - 4 in a Grassmannian of dimension 2 u(e) - 4 gives
   (codimension 4 at e = 5, 8 at e = 6). With EXP-006 (0.19 to 0.83 times q^(-2) at e = 4) the comparison holds over
   three degrees. [MV for the counts; the comparison is a heuristic]
4. Purity is again not read from the Hilbert function: 77 of the 120 spaces with the pure Hilbert function over GF(5)
   have another Betti table, 28 of 70 over GF(7), 23 of 63 over GF(3) at e = 6. [MV]

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- Every family ended at the early stop of 40 pure spaces, as the hypothesis provides (chunks already running
  finished, hence 43 and 42); the shares are over the spaces done.
- The summary generator of this experiment is loaded by path, because the one of EXP-006 has the same module name.
- The whole run took 29 minutes.

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on 60 pure spaces; the known answers of G1, among them the stored counts of EXP-006.
- Re-check by the coordinating session, without the routine of this experiment: the totals re-tallied from the stored
  chunks; fifteen pure spaces (five per family) rebuilt from their stored bases with the module construction of
  `uhlib` in pure Python, their Hilbert functions and Betti tables recomputed (all pure, lengths 125 and 216); one pair
  per family rebuilt from its printed text with a separate parser (all pure); the seed rule.

## How could this be wrong?

- As in EXP-006: a realization is one module whose table was computed in two systems and rebuilt a third time from
  the printed polynomials; the shared reading of the algebra is guarded by the known answers.
- The shares are estimates from samples that stopped early; they bear on the heuristic only.
- The statement "exactly in the characteristics other than 2" for e = 5 combines these modules with Theorems A and C.
