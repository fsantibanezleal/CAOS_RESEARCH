# EXP-013 verdict: CONFIRMED. In all ten cases the pull-back along the endomorphism has the Betti table of the module with every degree multiplied by d, in two instruments; the point (1,2,2,1) is realized on (0,8,24,32) in characteristic 3, on (0,12,36,48) in characteristic 5 and on (0,16,48,64) and (0,24,72,96) in characteristic 7

Date: 2026-11-20. Hypothesis frozen in commit c8a05607 before any computation of this experiment. (The hypothesis
file carries the date 2026-11-21 by a clerical error; it was written and frozen on 2026-11-20.) Exact arithmetic:
`uhlib` with a routine of this experiment for the substitution (first instrument), Singular 4.3.2 with Plural (second
instrument). Numbers: `results-summary.md` and `artifacts/results.json` (generated by `run.py table`).

## Gates

- G1 passed: each of the five pairs (phi(x), phi(y)) is pure with a central commutator; the modules M' are as the
  hypothesis says (the pair of quadrics over GF(3), the power pairs of degree 2 over GF(5), GF(7) and of degree 3

- G3 passed: 9 unit tests (among them phi(u v) = phi(u) phi(v) on random forms).

## What is established, and by what

1. The point (1, 2, 2, 1) of (0, 8, 24, 32) is realized over U in characteristic 3, by
   f = x^8 + y^8 + x^4 y^4 + 2 x^3 y^3 z, g = x^8 + x^4 y^4 + x^3 y^3 z + y^4 z^2 + 2 z^4: length 512, pure table.
   [MV, two instruments, and rebuilt by the coordinating session; the theorem of
   `context/2026-11-21-pull-back-and-scaling.md`, reviewed, proves it from the pure pair of quadrics over GF(3) and
   the pair x^4 + z^2, y^4 + z^2]
2. The point of (0, 12, 36, 48) is realized in characteristic 5 by f = (x^6 + z^3)^2, g = (y^6 + z^3)^2: length 1728.
   [MV, as above]
3. The points of (0, 16, 48, 64) and (0, 24, 72, 96) are realized in characteristic 7 by the squares and the cubes of
   x^8 + z^4, y^8 + z^4: lengths 4096 and 13824. [MV, two instruments]
4. In these four pairs (e, p) the characteristic divides neither e nor e - 1; the pure pairs come from pure pairs of
   degree 2 or 3 by an endomorphism of degree d = 0 or 1 modulo p.
5. The statement is not special to pairs of forms: three modules with tables that are not of that shape rescale in
   the same way. [MV]
6. The hypotheses are used: a substitution that is not an endomorphism of finite colength (N1), or not an
   endomorphism at all (N2), does not rescale the table. [MV]

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The three hours of the budget are counted from the start recorded in the results; the run took 27 minutes.
- The routine was written before its test file was complete; the failing run of the tests was reproduced
  afterwards by withholding the routine.
- S7 was computed under its 40-minute rule: the table of the first instrument took 18 minutes and that of the
  second 6.5 minutes.

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on all 20 modules; known answers in G1; two cases outside the hypotheses.
- Re-check by the coordinating session, without the routine of this experiment: S2, S3 and T1 rebuilt with a separate
  substitution (x -> phi(x), y -> phi(y), z -> phi(x) phi(y) - phi(y) phi(x), monomial by monomial), the Betti tables
  of M' and of its pull-back recomputed with the pure Python module construction of `uhlib`: the table is the scaled
  table and the length is d^3 times in the three cases (512, 1728, 81); 0 findings.
- The independent review of the proof computed the pairs of degree 8 over GF(3) and 12 over GF(5) with its own code,
  and six other cases, with the same outcome (record `bs-endomorphism-scaling-review-2026-11-21` in the coordination
  repository).

## How could this be wrong?

- A realization is one module whose table was computed in two systems and, for S2 and S3, rebuilt a third time.
- The experiment checks cases; the statement for every module and every d = 0 or 1 modulo p rests on the proof.
