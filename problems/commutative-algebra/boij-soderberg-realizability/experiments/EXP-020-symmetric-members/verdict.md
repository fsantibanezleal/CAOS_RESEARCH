## Deviations from the hypothesis

- None changes a count. The rank of alpha is counted from 0 among the nonzero alpha. The Betti tables are computed
  on the passing members in the order of their index whether or not the staged test passed (every passing member
  passed it). "Pure" was first computed without the length; the routine was changed to return the length from
  `uhlib` and the whole run made again, with the same counts; the first run was removed. The filter ran on 12
  processes and the tests in U on 6.
- The number of pure members is 5 per case by the stopping rule (5 pure members or 40 tables); the lists of passing
  members are complete.
- Time: 420 seconds accounted by the runner of the 3,600 allowed.

## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation repeated by a second system and a second construction.
- The statement "the two congruences hold together" is exact for the nine lists and is not proved; nothing in the
  settled pairs depends on it.
- A list without a passing member would have said nothing about the other members of the stratum; no list was
  empty.
