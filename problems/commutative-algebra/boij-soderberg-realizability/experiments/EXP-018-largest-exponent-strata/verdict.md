## What is established, and by what

1. The point (1, 2, 2, 1) of (0, e, 3e, 4e) is realized over U over the prime field at five pairs that were not
   settled. One pair for each (the summary prints three per case):
   - (11, 13): f = 6 x^11 + 6 x^3 y^8 + 9 x^2 y^7 z + 4 x y^6 z^2 + 8 y^5 z^3,
     g = 3 y^11 + 11 x^8 y^3 + 3 x^7 y^2 z + 3 x^6 y z^2 + 12 x^5 z^3;
   - (11, 17): f = 4 x^11 + 8 x^2 y^9 + 8 x y^8 z + 15 y^7 z^2, g = 9 y^11 + 16 x^9 y^2 + 13 x^8 y z + 11 x^7 z^2;
   - (13, 17): f = 11 x^13 + 11 x^3 y^10 + 2 x^2 y^9 z + 8 x y^8 z^2 + 9 y^7 z^3,
     g = 12 y^13 + x^9 y^2 z + 3 x^8 y z^2 + 11 x^7 z^3;
   - (13, 19): f = 5 x^13 + 5 x^2 y^11 + 18 x y^10 z + 10 y^9 z^2, g = 15 y^13 + x^11 y^2 + 12 x^10 y z + 6 x^9 z^2;
   - (14, 23): f = 3 x^14 + x^2 y^12 + 18 x y^11 z + 4 y^10 z^2, g = 2 y^14 + 20 x^12 y^2 + 16 x^11 y z + 18 x^10 z^2;
   of lengths 1331, 1331, 2197, 2197 and 2744, each with the pure table. [MV, two instruments, and rebuilt by the
   coordinating session] With the earlier results, at e = 11 every odd prime below 22 except 3 is settled.
2. The proposition on the weight-zero projection held where it was tested as a prediction: at each of the five
   pairs the stratum with the next exponent, where 2t >= p + 1, holds no member with the pure Hilbert function
   among 50,000, while the stratum with the largest allowed exponent holds pure pairs.
3. The share of members with the pure Hilbert function is of the order p^(-(e-t-2)), with a factor between 0.2 and
   0.7 in the seven cases: the number of conditions read off the working notes fits. This is an observation; the
   decomposition behind it is not written as a proof.
4. So for a pair (e, p) with p > e, the stratum to search is the one with the largest t of the parity of e with
   2t <= p - 1: it worked at the five pairs tried, with a sample of at most 1,500,000 members.

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The first run of family T stopped on a memory error in a worker process (twelve workers each kept up to 64
  tensors); the implementer reduced that cache to 4 entries in `run.py` and resumed. No count depends on it.
- Every case with pure members stopped early except (13, 17), which used its whole budget of 1,500,000. All cases
  are random samples (every stratum has more members than its budget).
- Time: 2,147 seconds accounted by the runner of the 7,200 allowed; 12 worker processes.

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on 21 pure members; two controls with known answers; five strata where a reviewed proposition

- Re-check by the coordinating session, without the routine of this experiment (script and log under the data
  root): the first two pure members of each of the seven cases with a pure member, rebuilt from the stored
  coefficient vectors with a separate parser, checked to lie in the stratum the case names, and tabled with the
  pure Python module construction of `uhlib`: 14 members, all pure, of lengths 729, 1331, 2197 and 2744. 0 findings.
- The cases and the predictions were frozen before any computation at these pairs, from a reviewed proposition
  (which exponents are excluded) and from working notes (how many conditions remain).

## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation repeated by a second system and a second construction.
- The absence in the strata of family Z is a sample of 50,000 per stratum; it is the proposition, not the sample,
  that says the strata hold no pure pair.
- The reading "e - t - 2 conditions" is supported by shares in seven cases; two of them rest on fewer than seven
  members.
