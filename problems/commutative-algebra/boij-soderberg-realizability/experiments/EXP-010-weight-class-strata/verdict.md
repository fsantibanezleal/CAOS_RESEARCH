## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation repeated by a second system and a second construction; the risk is in the
  statements of absence. An enumerated stratum without a pure member is a statement about that stratum over the
  prime field; a sampled one is "none found". Nothing is said about pairs without a cyclic symmetry of this kind or
  about extensions of the field.
- The shares are not estimates of a density: the early stop biases them, and the strata were chosen.
- P4 was a prediction from a probe on three pairs; it is now supported by a proof, which is the stronger ground.
