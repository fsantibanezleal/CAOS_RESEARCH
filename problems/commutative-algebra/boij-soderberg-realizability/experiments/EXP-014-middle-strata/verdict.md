## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation repeated by a second system and by a second construction; the risk is in the
  statements of absence. An enumerated stratum without a pure member is a statement about that stratum over the
  prime field; a sampled stratum without one ((9, 11), (9, 13), (12, 7)) is "none found" and nothing more.
- The shares of pure members are not estimates of anything beyond the cases run: the early stop biases them.
- The first verdict of gate G1 was a failure caused by the design of the gate; the amendment was written before any
  result of families V and W existed, and the frozen predictions were not changed.
