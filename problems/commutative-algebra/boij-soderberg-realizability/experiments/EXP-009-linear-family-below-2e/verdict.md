## What is established, and by what

1. e = 6 in characteristic 11. Over GF(11) the pair f = x^6 + y^4 z, g = y^6 + 3 x^4 z (the member (0, 1, 0, 3) of L_6)
   gives a module U/(Uf + Ug) of length 216 with the pure Betti table (1, 2, 2, 1) on (0, 6, 18, 24). With EXP-008
   (characteristic 3), Theorem A (characteristic 0 or at least 12) and Theorem C (characteristic 2): the point
   (1,2,2,1) of (0, 6, 18, 24) is realized over U in every characteristic other than 2, 5 and 7; it is not realized in
   characteristic 2, and characteristics 5 and 7 are open. [MV, two instruments, and rebuilt by the coordinating
   session]
2. e = 7 in characteristics 11 and 13. Over GF(11), f = x^7 + y^5 z, g = y^7 + 3 x^5 z; over GF(13), f = x^7 + y^5 z,
   g = y^7 + x^5 z: each gives a module of length 343 with the pure table on (0, 7, 21, 28). With Theorem A
   (characteristic 0 or at least 14) and Theorem C: the point of (0, 7, 21, 28) is realized in every characteristic
   other than 2, 3, 5 and 7; characteristics 3, 5 and 7 are open. [MV, as above]
3. Short pairs for the characteristics already settled by random search: over GF(7), x^4 + y^2 z, y^4 + x^2 z and
   x^5 + y^3 z, y^5 + x^3 z are pure; over GF(5), x^4 + y^2 z, y^4 - 2 x^2 z is pure. [MV]
4. The inclusion of L_e in the rank locus, proved in characteristic 0 or at least 2e, holds on every member in the
   fifteen pairs below 2e. This is an observation on finite fields, without a proof; the weight argument does not
   apply there. [MV]
5. Where the family fails. For p at most e (seven pairs) every member has rank below r, so the family contains no
   pure member in those characteristics. At (5,5) and (6,7) most members have rank r and none has the pure Hilbert
   function. Nothing follows for other pairs of forms: EXP-006 and EXP-008 found pure pairs at (4,3), (5,3), (5,5) and
   (6,3) outside this family. [MV]

## Deviations from the hypothesis

- The early stop was applied when the tasks already started had finished, so the pairs that stopped early examined
  160 to 240 members of rank r and found 95 to 160 pure members, more than the 40 of the hypothesis.
- The family is built in the routine of this experiment, because the constructor of EXP-007 takes integers and
  family Y needs GF(p^2); a unit test compares it with the printed polynomials.
- Family Y ran in nine pairs, the pairs with p < 2e in which family X found no pure member.
- The whole run took 28 minutes.

## Adversarial validation

- Two instruments on 55 pure members; known answers in G1, among them the control pairs where the proposition
  applies.
- Re-check by the coordinating session, without the routine of this experiment: six decisive members rebuilt from
  their printed polynomials with a separate parser, checked to be the members their parameters name, and their Betti
  tables recomputed with the module construction of `uhlib` in pure Python (all pure; lengths 216, 343, 343, 64, 64,
  125); the rank of mu_(3e) recomputed with python-flint for every member of (5,5), (6,5) and (4,3) and for 400 random
  members of (6,7) and (7,7): no member above r, 212 of rank r at (5,5) as stored, none at (6,5), (4,3) and (7,7), and
  153 of 400 at (6,7) against the stored 918 of 2,401.

## How could this be wrong?

- A realization is one module whose table was computed in two systems and rebuilt a third time from the printed
  polynomials.
- "No pure member" at (5,5) and (6,7) rests on the first instrument: the Hilbert function of every member of rank r
  over GF(p), and of every member of rank r in a sample over GF(p^2); members over larger extensions were not
  examined. It is a statement about this family over those fields.
- For the pairs that stopped early the counts of pure members are counts among the members examined, in
  lexicographic order of the parameters; they are not shares of the family.
