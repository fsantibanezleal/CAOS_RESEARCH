## How could this be wrong?

- A pure pair is a finite computation on two explicit polynomials over GF(3); three programs agree on it (the
  numpy tables of the experiment, Singular, the pure Python construction). An error would have to be common to the
  definition of U in all three, which the earlier experiments and the controls share and test.
- 'Every pair with e <= 16 is settled' rests on the earlier verdicts for the other pairs; this experiment adds only
  (11, 3).
- The counts by stage depend on the block pattern of the note on syzygy classes (reviewed); the control at (5, 3)
  reproduces the 400 pure pairs found without the pattern. A pure pair of S(5; 0, 1) without the pattern cannot exist
  by that proposition; one in S(2; 0, 1) is outside this experiment.
- Nothing is said about fields larger than GF(3), about S(2; 0, 1), or about e >= 17.
