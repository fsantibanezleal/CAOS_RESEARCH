# 2. Known results and scope

The original boundary $n=k\alpha$ already contains $(6,2,3)$. Thus EXP-001's numerical extension starts at $k=4$, although its theorem and construction also include $k=3$. The [primary dossier](../context/references.md) records scope and novelty searches.

Das-Gupta prove $f(2k-1,k-1,k)=k^2-1$, $k\ge4$, with full joins of independent sets and trees as extremals. [EXP-001](../experiments/EXP-001-tree-strip/verdict.md) proves $f(2k,k-1,k)=k^2$. Hence the optimum increases by one and the gap above the degree bound vanishes. The original discovery and preceding-boundary classification remain external results.

The [full-shell result](../experiments/EXP-002-next-shell/proof.md) includes three known overlaps: $\alpha=k+1$ has $n=2\alpha$; $(n,\alpha,k)=(6,2,3)$ has $n=k\alpha$; and $\alpha=k-1$ is EXP-001. The original large-order result adds no other overlap on this shell. The new equality classification for $\alpha=2$ must not be described as a classification of all shell extremals. See the [September 5 portfolio refresh](../context/2026-09-05-portfolio-refresh.md) for the bounded novelty audit.

## EXP-003: precise novelty and overlap

Ahanjideh, Ekim, and Yildiz's final 2024 paper poses the intermediate triangle-free matching conjecture. Banak, Ekim, and Taskin's 2023 work already provides the attaining construction, and its author-manuscript Table 3 certifies $T(d,d+1)=d^2+d+2$ for $d=7,\ldots,12$. At $(d,m)=(13,14)$, the table leaves the interval 184 to 185. EXP-003 proves the uniform upper bound for every $d\ge7$, giving $T(13,14)=184$. It claims neither the construction nor the earlier finite values as new. See the [source-complete review](../context/2026-09-05-next-matching-review.md) and [proof](../experiments/EXP-003-triangle-free-next-matching/proof.md).

The proof includes a direct Tutte-Berge argument and an alternative using the published AEY component reduction. It settles one complete matching level, not all intermediate levels or the full conjecture. Its Bougard-Joret consequence is a one-edge bracket with an unbounded gap above the degree-sum prediction; the attaining triangle-free graph cannot itself be complemented into a graph with the required connectivity.

The separate next-matching manuscript v0.01 is [published at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22343022](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22343022), concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22343021, with fresh public-byte and latest-version verification recorded in its [receipt](../../../../manuscripts/bougard-joret/next-matching/publication-verification.json). Scoped integration remains pending. The published first-shell manuscript v0.02 is preserved.
