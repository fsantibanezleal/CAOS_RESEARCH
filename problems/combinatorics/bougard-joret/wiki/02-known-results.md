# 2. Known results and scope

The original boundary $n=k\alpha$ already contains $(6,2,3)$. Thus our numerical range starts at $k=4$, although the theorem and construction also include $k=3$. The [primary dossier](../context/references.md) records scope and novelty searches.

Das-Gupta prove $f(2k-1,k-1,k)=k^2-1$, $k\ge4$, with full joins of independent sets and trees as extremals. [EXP-001](../experiments/EXP-001-tree-strip/verdict.md) proves $f(2k,k-1,k)=k^2$. Hence the optimum increases by one and the gap above the degree bound vanishes. The original discovery and preceding-boundary classification remain external results.
