## Selection and evidence standard

Continue with the full Bougard-Joret next shell, `n = alpha + k + 1`, using the already established adjacent tree strip as a baseline. A full-shell theorem would extend that result; the strip alone does not prove the shell. The original conjecture has already been disproved, so this campaign targets a corrected, precisely stated restricted problem. Literature searches establish an identified gap, not absolute priority or proof that no prior result exists.

`[P]` means a primary abstract, theorem statement, or specified passage was checked online; it does not mean the complete external proof was independently verified. `[A]` identifies abstract-only access. `[L]` denotes existing local evidence. Suggested targets are research judgments, not established novelty. No external computational certificate was replayed in this literature lane.

## Alternative priorities and overlap controls

1. Hoa: one exact connected-noncomplete-restgraph witness would answer a stated open question; certificate size and gadget search remain risks.
2. Boij-Soderberg: minimum counterexample degree has a finite candidate surface, but unresolved noncommutative realizability and prior local discoveries must be separated.
3. Sphere energy: analytic Hopf-lifted Diamond energy has a stated literature gap, with an important change of sphere dimension.
4. BSD: explain constrained-family bias or enlarge a proven family; do not duplicate the existing conductor census.

None of these presently displaces the full Bougard-Joret next shell on immediate proof feasibility. This is a resource and evidence judgment, not a ranking of mathematical importance or a promise of a solution.

## Search boundaries

The refresh compared the existing report before searching. Queries targeted newly available partial results, exact theorem scopes, specific family restrictions, later versions, and disconfirming overlaps. Primary full texts were consulted for the most consequential RH and BSD statements; primary abstracts and author manuscripts supplied the other explicitly labeled scopes. Search snippets from aggregators were discovery aids only. No claim that a famous conjecture is solved rests on an unverified preprint, press account, or finite numerical calculation. A failed search is not a proof of absence.
