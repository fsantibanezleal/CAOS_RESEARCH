## Decision

Select the entire next matching level of the triangle-free degree/matching extremal problem, reached through Bougard-Joret graph complementation. The targeted statement is $T(d,d+1)=d^2+d+2$ for every integer $d\ge7$. It is a precise slice of the published Ahanjideh-Ekim-Yildiz Conjecture 6.1. Banak-Ekim-Taskin already give the attaining construction and exact values through $d=12$ on this slice. Their Table 3 leaves $T(13,14)$ between 184 and 185. The proposed contribution is a uniform upper-bound proof, not a newly invented construction or a rerun of their integer programs.

The initial second-shell Bougard idea was displaced by this sharper opportunity. A regular-complement obstruction alone follows from classical Andrasfai-Erdos-Sos and Regular Mantel theorems, so it is insufficient as a new regular-graph theorem. Moreover, the known triangle-free extremizer's complement fails the required connectivity. EXP-003 explicitly preserves that rejected shortcut and proves only the Bougard bracket it actually supports. The first-shell preprint v0.02 remains correct and unchanged.

### Why the alternatives rank lower

Hoa Question 3 is the strongest fallback: one exactly certified maximal non-Hamiltonian graph with a longest cycle leaving a connected noncomplete induced remainder would answer it. Question 2 instead permits a disconnected remainder with a noncomplete component. The two are not interchangeable. Bullock et al.'s Theorems 3.12 and 3.13 have different disjoint/equal attachment-neighborhood and traceability conditions. In the local three-lobe route, the best detour omits the minimum of the three endpoint deficiencies. Thus all three gadgets need deficiency at least three; changing only the middle lobe while retaining deficiency-two endpoints cannot work. This is a local construction inference, not a general nonexistence theorem. [Bullock's thesis](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.12086) is dated June 2004 despite its July 2025 upload.

For Boij, the local degree-ten pair remains a useful smaller counterexample, but minimum degree ten is unproved because lower-degree realizability cases remain. Erman-Sam already anticipate sp4/Kostant constructions, creating prior-art risk for simply exhibiting that Heisenberg family. A primitive nonrealization does not exclude its multiples. Sam's author homepage was inaccessible during the follow-up, so that publication-tracing gap remains explicit.
