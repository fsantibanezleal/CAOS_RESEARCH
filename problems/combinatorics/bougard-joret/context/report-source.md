# Portfolio review and research selection, 4 September 2026

Audience: CAOS Research. Scope: all 20 rows of `program/portfolio.yaml` at integration commit `f82680d`, comprising 14 unstarted and six active problems. Sources were accessed online on 2026-09-04. This is a decision-oriented primary-source review, not an exhaustive bibliography of six Millennium problems. Mathematical status and repository lifecycle are different: `published` means surfaced, not solved.

## Decision and result

Select the Bougard-Joret adjacent exceptional strip. It has an elementary exact verifier, a recent primary boundary result, a clean unbounded parameter, and no active dedicated worktree. [EXP-001](../experiments/EXP-001-tree-strip/verdict.md) now proves $f(2k,k-1,k)=k^2$ for every $k\ge3$ and characterizes all extremals by nonstar trees. The new numerical range starts at $k=4$; the broader problem remains unresolved. This selection was based on tractability and a specific gap, not on a promise to solve a famous conjecture.

The most important literature correction concerns unsplittable flow: its July ledger predates an August disproof of the cost-free two-sided conjecture and stronger source-reported lower bounds. These do not invalidate the repository's exact finite calculations; they invalidate treating those calculations as the current literature frontier.

## Selection comparison

Bougard-Joret has a short uniform proof target and a full extremal characterization. Huneke-Wiegand has greater established infrastructure but a nontrivial symbolic source-chain gap and existing ownership. Petersen has a possible defect-composition route but needs an exact new statement. Hoa offers constructive opportunities with a heavier Hamiltonicity certificate. Boij-Soderberg has substantial overlap with prior local scouting. The other targets either have a large uniformity gap, unresolved statement scope, or no concrete new certificate surface.

The Bougard-Joret target passes the repository's nine-part radar: exact verifier 2; bounded certification 2 (a finite proof plus bounded refutation controls, not an exhaustive bound on all graphs); construction language 2; independent route 2; controls 2; primary scope 2; novelty window 1 (search cannot certify priority); compute fit 2; extension value 2. Total 17/18, a decision aid rather than a mathematical score.

## Search and stopping record

Two distinct research lanes reviewed the unstarted specialized topics and the active-topic/Millennium status sources. The coordinator reread the highest-consequence Bougard-Joret, unsplittable-flow and Huneke-Wiegand sources. Searches combined exact conjecture names, 2025/2026, counterexample, newest version, and the specific proposed next-boundary expressions. The Bougard-Joret final sections and adverse cases were read before computation.

Discovery stopped after every portfolio row had a primary status/source entry, competing targets had explicit gaps, and targeted searches no longer changed the selected theorem's scope. This is not a guarantee that no newer paper exists. Restricted-access abstracts, untested external certificates, and the six status-only Millennium checks are explicitly marked. Further literature review is required before launching any alternative target or broadening the theorem.
