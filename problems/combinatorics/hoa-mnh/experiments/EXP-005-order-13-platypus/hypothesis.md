# EXP-005 preflight: order 13 without a universal vertex, through the platypus generator (HMB-006, case B)

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: frozen before any computation with the generator. CPU only.

## Question

Which maximal non-Hamiltonian (MNH) graphs of order 13 have maximum degree below 12 (case B of the split used in
EXP-002 and EXP-004), and does Hoa's conjecture hold for them?

## Method

Lemma 2 of Goedgebeur, Neyt and Zamfirescu (arXiv:1712.05158, after Zamfirescu): an MNH graph G is a platypus
(non-Hamiltonian, every vertex-deleted subgraph traceable) if and only if Delta(G) < |V(G)| - 1. So the case-B MNH
graphs of order 13 are exactly the MNH graphs among the platypuses of order 13. The platypuses are generated by
GenHypohamiltonian 1.2.1 (Goedgebeur and Zamfirescu, https://caagt.ugent.be/hypoham/, option `platypus`, split by
`mod <rest> <modulo>`), converted from multicode to graph6 by `mc2g6.c`, and passed to EXP-001's `mnh` filter
(exact Hamiltonicity tests, every longest-cycle vertex set, residual completeness).

The 2-connectivity lemma recorded in HMB-006 and the degree-sum condition are not used as filters here: the
generator's list is complete for platypuses, and the MNH filter decides the rest.

## Gates (before production)

1. The generator reproduces GNZ Table 1: 48, 814 and 24,847 platypuses at orders 10, 11 and 12.
2. The MNH filter on those lists gives EXP-002's case-B counts: 4, 10 and 37 at orders 10, 11 and 12.
3. EXP-001's `verify.py` re-checks every order-13 graph found.

## Predictions

- The number of case-B MNH graphs of order 13 lies between 50 and 150 (case B: 4, 10, 37 at orders 10 to 12)
  (LOW to MODERATE).
- None is a counterexample to Hoa's conjecture (HIGH).
