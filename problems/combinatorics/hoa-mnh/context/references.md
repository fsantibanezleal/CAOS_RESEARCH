Problem 266 [V] (p. 319): "A nonhamiltonian graph G is called maximal if G becomes Hamiltonian by adding any new
edge. Question. Let G be a maximal nonhamiltonian graph and C a longest cycle in G. Is the subgraph induced on
V(G) - V(C) complete?" Posed by Vu Dinh Hoa as a question; Zhan calls it a conjecture. The Erdos attribution
seen in one web summary is not supported by the primary text. Not obtained: Hoa 2005 (World Scientific, pp. 67-70,
