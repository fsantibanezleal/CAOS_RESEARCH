  structural upper bound and EXP-059's exact pinned determinant carry that
  conclusion.
- Generic inconsistency does not control the exceptional closed locus. A
  uniform all-parameter claim still requires a unit augmented-minor ideal or an
  equivalent exact stratified certificate.
