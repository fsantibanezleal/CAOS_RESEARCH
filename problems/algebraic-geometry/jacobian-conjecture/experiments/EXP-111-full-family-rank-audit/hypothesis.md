# EXP-111 - Full-family rank audit and correction of EXP-110

## Question

Does EXP-110 measure the complete reduced GGHV coefficient system, and is its
rank-\(124\) observation a new family-wide obstruction or a structural feature
already forced by the allowed \(Q\)-space?

## Motivation

EXP-110 reports rank \(124\) for \(M(\varepsilon)\) and rank \(125\) for the
augmented matrix at the pinned point and three random points. It then redirects
the programme toward proving that every \(125\)-minor of \(M\) vanishes.
Before spending machine time on that target, the matrix orientation, row pool,
and effective column count must be audited against the canonical EXP-071
construction.

Two cheap invariants suggest that the proposed target may be vacuous:

1. the \(Q\)-polygon contains the constant monomial, and
   \([P,1]=0\) for every \(P\);
2. EXP-110 builds its row list only from the forced polynomial \(P_T\), although
   lower-family directions may create additional bracket rows.

## Premise dependencies

- [MV] EXP-071 supplies the canonical \(Q\)-polygon, lower-family directions,
  pool predicate, and bracket coefficient map.
- [MV] EXP-059 and EXP-101 persist exact augmented minors that are nonzero at
  the pinned point.
- [D] A zero constant column gives
  \(\operatorname{rank}M(\varepsilon)\leq124\) identically.
- [D] One nonzero augmented \(125\)-minor at one parameter point proves that
  the augmented matrix has generic rank \(125\), but does not prove rank \(125\)
  at every parameter point.

## Predictions

1. [MV] The constant \(Q\)-column is identically zero for the forced polynomial
   and every one of the 51 lower directions.
2. [MV] The complete union of output rows is strictly larger than EXP-110's
   forced-only row list.
3. [MV] Adding the omitted rows leaves the pinned and deterministic random
   profiles at rank \(124/125\) for \(M/[M\mid b]\).
4. [D] The proposed uniform vanishing of \(125\)-minors of \(M\) follows
   immediately from the constant column and is not the remaining research
   target.
5. [D] The correct target is the common zero locus of augmented
   \(125\)-minors, including recomputation with the complete row union on
   exceptional strata.

## Method

- Import the exact EXP-071 construction.
- Compare the forced-only row set with the union generated by all lower
  directions.
- Build both matrices in the correct orientation: output rows by \(Q\)
  coefficient columns.
- Assert the zero constant column symbolically, direction by direction.
- Compute modular ranks at the pinned point and three fixed-seed rational
  points using two primes.
- Cross-check the pinned augmented-rank result against the exact persisted
  EXP-059 certificate statement.

## One-sidedness

- A PASS proves the structural zero column, the row-pool omission, and the
  stated modular rank profiles. Together with the already persisted exact
  pinned augmented minor, it proves generic inconsistency on a nonempty
  Zariski-open subset. It does not prove inconsistency on the exceptional
  closed locus or close \((72,108)\).
- A FAIL refutes at least one proposed correction and blocks any plan rewrite
  until the mismatch is explained. Random full rank \(125\) for \(M\) would
  specifically refute the constant-column construction or the matrix
  orientation.

## Adversarial validation

Use two independent large primes, include the pinned point, compare forced-only
and complete row unions, and assert the constant column directly from every
coefficient matrix rather than inferring it from rank.

## Invariant-first note

The constant-column identity is the cheapest distinguishing invariant. It
decides the maximal-minor question before determinant interpolation or
Schwartz-Zippel testing. Row-union completeness is the second invariant because
an omitted equation can invalidate a claimed complete system even when sampled
ranks happen to agree.
