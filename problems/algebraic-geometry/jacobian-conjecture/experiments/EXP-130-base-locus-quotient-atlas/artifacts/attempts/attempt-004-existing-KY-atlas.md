The base locus must be tested in \(K[Y]\), not only in \(K\), because
\(Y=A^2C\) is free when \(R=S=0\). Five already reconstructed maximal-minor
sections were reduced in every exact CRT block.

Results:

- degree 69 is covered by the constant EXP-124 section;
- degree 12 is covered by the affine pair EXP-125-h36 and
  EXP-129-atlas-1, whose \(Y\)-resultant is a unit;
- degrees 3 and 6 are not covered by any constant section or affine pair in
  the existing atlas.

Every accepted pair certificate has an exact nonzero multiplication norm and
a verified inverse modulo the block factor. Therefore only nine normalized
base points, in blocks of degrees 3 and 6, require new row selection.
