# EXP-136 - Cascade dimension audit: which ranks, indices and groups the located implications decide

- **Question.** EXP-016 and EXP-018 located the implications of the consequence cascade and
  recorded that "only the implication DIRECTIONS carry weight". The statuses derived from them
  are, however, dimension-specific: Mathieu "false for SU(N), N >= 3"; Dixmier "rank 2 false;
  rank 1 open"; Poisson "as with Dixmier, where rank 1 stays open"; Gaussian moments "false at
  some finite dimension". For each row, which dimensions (ranks, indices, groups) do the
  implications decide when every implication is taken with the dimension indices stated in its
  primary source?
- **Motivation.** Methodology 03 (claims imported from the literature are re-verified against the
  primary record, here at the level of indices, not only directions). The Dixmier row reads
  JC(4) false as deciding Dixmier(2), although the located implication runs from JC(4) to
  Dixmier(2); the other dimension-specific rows were never audited at the level of indices. A
  source post-dating EXP-018 bears on the Gaussian moments row (C. D. Long, arXiv:2607.18186,
  2026-07-20).
- **Falsifiable predictions** (literature experiment with an exact computational part):
  1. **Dixmier.** Belov-Kanel and Kontsevich (arXiv:math/0512171, introduction and Theorem 1)
     state DC(n) => JC(n) (the well-known direction) and JC(2n) => DC(n). With JC(1) true and
     JC(n) false exactly for n >= 3 (EXP-001; JC(2) open), the closure of these implications
     decides DC(n) false for every n >= 3 and leaves DC(1) and DC(2) undecided. If the closure
     decides DC(2), EXP-016 item 5 stands.
  2. **Poisson.** Adjamagbo and van den Essen (arXiv:math/0608009, Theorem 7) state, for each n,
     JC(2n) => PC(n) => DC(n) => JC(n), where PC(n) concerns the canonical Poisson algebra in 2n
     variables. The closure decides PC(n) false for every n >= 3 and leaves PC(1) and PC(2)
     undecided.
  3. **Gaussian moments.** Long gives explicit P, Q in three variables, so GMC(n) is false for
     every n >= 3; Derksen, van den Essen and Zhao (arXiv:1506.05192, Proposition 4.2) prove
     GMC(1); GMC(2) is open.
  4. **Mathieu.** The located proof of Mathieu's implication (K. Zwart, arXiv:2511.16561,
     Theorems 4.16 and 4.23, Corollary 4.24) establishes, for each N, that the Mathieu conjecture
     for SU(N) implies the invertibility of every Keller map x - h of C^N with h homogeneous;
     general maps are reached only through the Bass-Connell-Wright reduction (Theorem 4.4 there),
     which raises the dimension. (a) F (EXP-001) is not affinely equivalent to a map x - h with h
     homogeneous, so the located proof does not decide SU(3) through F. (b) The 24-variable
     cubic-homogeneous form verified in EXP-041 is of that type, so the Mathieu conjecture is
     false for SU(N), N >= 24.
- **Method.** The primary texts are fetched (arXiv PDFs and abstract pages) and the load-bearing
  statements transcribed into `artifacts/sources.md` with their locations (section, theorem,
  PDF text line). `run.py` (exact, sympy, CPU):
  - (A) closure of the located implications over JC(n), DC(n), PC(n) for n = 1..8 from the
    ground facts JC(1) true, JC(n) false for n >= 3, JC(2) unknown, with the monotonicity
    JC(n) => JC(m), DC(n) => DC(m) for n > m (Belov-Kanel and Kontsevich, introduction);
  - (B) the ideal generated by all second partial derivatives of the components of F. A map
    A o (x - h) o B (A, B affine bijections, h homogeneous of degree d >= 2) has total degree d
    and a Taylor expansion with no terms of degree 2..d-1 at the point B^-1(0); F has total
    degree 7, so an empty zero set (Groebner basis [1]) shows F is not of that form;
  - (C) the EXP-041 input (Thompson's H on Q^24, read from `EXP-041-gradient-witness/data/`
    after a SHA-256 match with EXP-041's record): each component of H is homogeneous cubic, the
    map is x + H with identity linear part, and the published collision holds exactly.
  - `artifacts/result.json` records the closure table and the three checks; the run exits
    nonzero if any check fails.
- **What a PASS proves and what a FAIL proves.** The closure decides a statement only from the
  located implications; "undecided" means undecided by these implications, not a claim about
  the literature at large (the verdict records one bounded search pass for further results on
  DC(1), DC(2), PC(1), PC(2), GMC(2) and SU(N), 3 <= N <= 23). Check B is two-sided for its own
  claim (F is, or is not, affinely of homogeneous type). For 3 <= N <= 23, a PASS of 4(a) shows
  only that the located proof does not reach those groups through F; it proves nothing about
  the truth of the Mathieu conjecture there.
- **Premise dependencies.** JC(n) false for n >= 3: EXP-001 (confirmed). The 24-variable form:
  EXP-041 (confirmed: homogeneity, exact collision, nilpotency of JH). The implication
  statements: the primary texts named above, located in this experiment. Mathieu's 1997 paper
  (Seminaires et Congres 2, SMF) was not located online; its content enters only through
  Zwart's exposition and the statements of later authors (flagged in the verdict).
- **Compute budget and kill criterion.** A: under 1 s. B: Groebner basis of 18 polynomials of
  degree <= 5 in 3 variables, target 10 s, gate 120 s; if gated, the direct certificate
  (d2F3/dxdy = -6x forces x = 0, d2F3/dx2 = -6y - 6xz then forces y = 0, and d2F1/dy2 = 8 at
  x = 0) is checked by substitution instead. C: parsing and two exact evaluations of a
  24-component map, under 30 s.
- **Invariant-first note.** For B the deciding invariants are the total degree of F and the
  order of vanishing of its nonlinear Taylor part at the candidate base point; together they
  decide the question outright, so no search over affine changes is needed. A needs no
  invariant (finite closure). No invariant of F decides whether some other three-dimensional
  counterexample is of homogeneous type; that question stays open and is not attempted.
- **Success criterion.** Each of 1-4 resolved (confirmed or refuted) with its located statement
  and the run output; the affected rows of EXP-016 (items 1, 2, 5) and of the EXP-018 status
  board restated in the verdict.
- **Failure criterion.** A located statement differs from the transcription used here, or a run
  check fails.
