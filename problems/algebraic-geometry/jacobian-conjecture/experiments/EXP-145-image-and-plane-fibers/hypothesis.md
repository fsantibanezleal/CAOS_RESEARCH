# EXP-145 - The image of the announced map and its fibers over the plane C = 0, exact

Date: 2026-11-14. Frozen before any code of this experiment is written or run.

- **Question.** For the announced map `F = (u^3 z + y^2 u (4 + 3xy), y + 3x u^2 z + 3x y^2 (4 + 3xy), 2x - 3x^2 y - x^3 z)`,
  `u = 1 + xy` (EXP-001), is the image all of `C^3` (and of `R^3` for real targets), and what are the fibers over the
  plane `C = 0`?
- **Motivation.** Two recorded statements are contradicted by a derivation in the foundational v0.15 draft
  (CAOS_MANAGE `wip/caos-research/jc-foundational-revision-2026-11-14.md`, "Additional corrections"):
  - EXP-011 verdict item 4 records `F|_{R^3}` as surjective. The fiber polynomial `W(w) = 4(w^2 - w^3) - wBC + AC^2`
    has a triple root exactly when `BC = 4/3` and `AC^2 = 4/27`; then no simple root exists and the fiber would be
    empty, so the image would miss the curve `Gamma = {(4/(27c^2), 4/(3c), c) : c != 0}`. The EXP-011 grid never meets
    `Gamma`.
  - EXP-007 verdict item 4 records the plane `C = 0` as asymptotic, with one point over generic `(A, B, 0)`. The
    draft derives a three-point fiber when `B^2 != 16A` (the flat-sheet point and two points with
    `x^2 (B^2 - 16A) = 4`); EXP-007 kept only rational solutions, and the two points are irrational unless `B^2 - 16A`
    is a rational square.
  A check by the coordinating session (sympy, 2026-11-14, not archived) found the Groebner basis of the fiber over
  `Gamma(c)` with `c` inverted to be `[1]`, and the three points over `(0, 1, 0)`. This experiment archives exact
  computations so that the corrected statements can carry machine-verified marks; EXP-007 item 4 and EXP-011 item 4 are
  superseded by it where they disagree (methodology 02: superseded experiments are not edited).

## Falsifiable predictions (exact rational arithmetic, sympy; the fiber ideal of a target t is (F - t))

1. **P1, the missing curve.** In `Q[x, y, z, c, v]`, the ideal generated by `27c^2 F_1 - 4`, `3c F_2 - 4`, `F_3 - c` and
   `cv - 1` has reduced Groebner basis `[1]` (lex order): no point of `C^3` maps to `Gamma(c)` for any `c != 0`.
2. **P2, everything else is hit.** (a) Symbolic: on `x = 0`, `F(0, y, z) = (z + 4y^2, y, 0)`, so every target with
   `C = 0` has a preimage. (b) At 40 rational targets with `C != 0` off `Gamma` (seed 20261114: 20 random, 10 on the
   discriminant surface `Sigma = 0` built from a prescribed double root, 10 within `10^-3` of a point of `Gamma`), the fiber
   ideal is not the unit ideal and its zero-dimensional quotient has dimension 1 to 3 (counted with multiplicity), and a
   real preimage exists when the target is real (Sturm count of the real roots of `W` plus reconstruction).
3. **P3, the plane C = 0.** (a) Symbolic: with `y = (Bx - 6)/(4x)` and `z = (26 - 3Bx)/(4x^2)`,
   `F = (B^2/16 - 1/(4x^2), B, 0)`, which equals `(A, B, 0)` exactly on `x^2 (B^2 - 16A) = 4`. (b) At 20 rational targets
   `(A, B, 0)` with `B^2 != 16A`, the fiber ideal has a zero-dimensional quotient of dimension 3 with three distinct
   points (radical), equal to the flat-sheet point and the two points of the parametrization; at 5 targets with
   `B^2 = 16A` the quotient has dimension 1 (the flat-sheet point only). (c) The fiber over `(0, 1, 0)` is
   `{(0, 1, -4), (2, -1/2, 5/4), (-2, 1, 2)}` and over `(-1/4, 0, 0)` the three announced points.
4. **Controls.** C1: replacing `4/27` by `5/27` in P1 must give a basis different from `[1]` (the perturbed curve is in
   the image). C2: the targets of EXP-007 item 4, read from its artifacts, have three complex points each, of which
   exactly one is rational, which explains the recorded "one point".

## What PASS and FAIL prove

- PASS: `F` is not surjective over `C` nor over `R`; its image is the complement of `Gamma` (P1 exactly, P2(a)
  symbolically for `C = 0`, and for `C != 0` the simple-root argument of the paper, [D], with P2(b) as machine-verified
  samples); the plane `C = 0` carries three-point fibers off `B^2 = 16A` (P3, symbolic identity plus samples). EXP-011
  item 4 and EXP-007 item 4 are superseded where they disagree.
- FAIL of P1: the fiber over some point of `Gamma` is nonempty; the draft's image statement is wrong and is withdrawn.
- FAIL of P3(b) or P2(b): a counterexample to the draft's fiber count or image statement, reported as found.

## Invariant-first note

The discriminant of `W` decides where roots merge but not whether a simple root survives; the triple-root locus (P1) is
the deciding invariant for the image, and the flat sheet `x = 0` (P2(a)) is the cheapest certificate of preimages over
`C = 0`.
