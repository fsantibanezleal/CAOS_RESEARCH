## Reduction [D]

u is a unit of K (H(0) != 0). The K-algebra automorphism q_i -> u^(-w_i) q_i of K[q0..q5] sends equation e to
u^(c_e) times a polynomial E'_e whose coefficients are polynomials in v and 1/v, i.e. lie in L; v is a unit (h(0)
!= 0), so E'_e can be scaled by a power of v into L[q] with polynomial coefficients in v. The ideal (E_1..E_13) of
K[q] is the unit ideal iff (E'_1..E'_13) is, and an ideal generated by polynomials over the subfield L is the unit
ideal over K iff it is over L (a Groebner basis over L is one over K). Hence Case 1 is empty iff

    I' = ( E'_1, ..., E'_13, h(v) )  in  Q[q0, ..., q5, v]

is the unit ideal, which Singular `std` over Q decides exactly (I' = (1) iff no root of h carries a solution).

## Gates

2. The transformation is checked exactly: for random rational points (q, u) with H(u) evaluated symbolically in
   K, E_e(q) equals u^(c_e) v^(-T_e) E'_e(u^(w) q) as elements of K (python-flint arithmetic in K).
