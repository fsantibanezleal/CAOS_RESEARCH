--- PREMISE SCRUTINY (declared discipline: check before claiming) ---

[1] The Krylov bound I declared: 'a finite covector, if it exists, exists at
    eps-degree <= dim V_inf (=122)'. Its derivation assumed the PINNED-sigma
    ladder: Lambda_alpha = (words in A_i = sigma T_i) applied to Lambda_0, so
    joint nilpotency of the A_i on V_inf would bound the degree.
    BUT EXP-064 REFUTED joint nilpotency for that sigma (the descending chain
    STABILIZES NONZERO at dim 39). So the pinned ladder does NOT terminate,
    and this bound DOES NOT APPLY. My declared prediction 1 is REFUTED as a
    route to a ceiling: it is conditional on a termination we know fails.
    (It does not follow that no covector exists: sigma carries a 165-dim gauge
    freedom per solve, so a different gauge could still terminate.)

[2] Is there a sigma-FREE a priori bound? The direct systems (EXP-067/070/
    072/073) at degree d have unknowns = covector coefficients Lambda_alpha,
    |alpha| <= d (about C(51+d,d)*165) and conditions = order-(d+1) blocks
    (multi-indices of size d+1, 125 rows each). Both grow; Noetherianity gives
    that the chain of solvable degrees stabilizes but NOT an effective bound.
    We have NO a priori ceiling. Prediction 3 (the decision-procedure
    statement) is therefore NOT established. Recorded as an honest negative.

--- WHAT THE SCRUTINY DOES YIELD (a clean structural correction) ---

[3] MONOTONICITY: the set of degrees at which a covector exists is UPWARD
    CLOSED. A degree-d covector is also a degree-(d+1) covector (take the
    top-order coefficients to be zero). Hence exactly one of:
      (a) no covector at ANY finite degree, or
      (b) there is a MINIMAL degree d0, and every d >= d0 works.
    Our decided facts: degree 1 INFEASIBLE (EXP-067), degree 2 INFEASIBLE
    (EXP-072). Therefore d0 >= 3 if case (b) holds.

[4] THE FRAMING CORRECTION (important for how our own sweeps are read):
    the support-restricted sweeps (pairs/triples/quadruples at degree 3) test
    NECESSARY conditions only. Every support subsystem being FEASIBLE does
    NOT establish that a degree-3 covector EXISTS; it only fails to refute it.
    Existence requires CONSTRUCTION (solving the full joint system), which is
    route R2. So the degree-3 'open' status is genuinely open in both
    directions, and no amount of passing sweeps can close it positively.
    Only a hit (infeasible support) decides it, negatively.

RESULT: prediction 1 REFUTED (the Krylov ceiling is conditional on a
  termination that EXP-064 refuted); prediction 3 NOT ESTABLISHED (no a priori
  sigma-free bound). Gained instead: upward-closedness of the feasible-degree

  (they can only refute, never confirm). The constructive route R2 is the ONLY
