# EXP-078 - Verdict: the finite-ceiling premise is REFUTED; a published claim is withdrawn

**Status: my own declared prediction REFUTED by scrutiny. The experiment's value is
a correction, including to an already-published manuscript claim.**

## Results (artifacts/output-2026-07-24.txt)

1. **[REFUTED]** The declared Krylov ceiling ("a finite covector, if it exists,
   exists at eps-degree <= dim V_inf = 122") presupposes that the pinned-sigma
   corrector ladder TERMINATES: only then is Lambda_alpha a word in the A_i
   applied to Lambda_0, bounded by joint nilpotency. EXP-064 MEASURED that this
   ladder does NOT terminate (the descending chain stabilizes nonzero at dim 39).
   The bound therefore does not apply. (Nor does its failure imply no covector:
   sigma has a 165-dim gauge freedom per solve.)
2. **[NOT ESTABLISHED]** No sigma-free a priori bound is available. In the direct
   systems both unknowns and conditions grow with the degree; Noetherianity gives
   stabilization of the solvable-degree chain but is not effective. We have NO
   degree ceiling, so the campaign is NOT (yet) a finite decision procedure.

## What the scrutiny DID yield

3. **[MV, structural]** The set of degrees admitting a covector is UPWARD CLOSED
   (a degree-d covector is a degree-(d+1) covector with zero top coefficients).
   So either no covector exists at any degree, or there is a minimal d0 and all
   larger degrees work. With degrees 1 (EXP-067) and 2 (EXP-072) closed, d0 >= 3.
4. **[MV, framing correction]** Our support-restricted sweeps are ONE-SIDED: they
   test necessary conditions. All supports passing does NOT establish existence of
   a covector at that degree; only an infeasible support decides, negatively.
   Hence the degree-3 "open" status cannot be closed positively by any amount of
   sweeping; the constructive route (R2, solve the full joint system) is the only
   way to a positive answer.
