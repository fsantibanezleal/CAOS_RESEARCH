# EXP-129 - An alternative maximal-minor section on the F7 CRT divisor

## Question

Can one exact maximal minor, distinct from (h_7) and (h_{36}), be selected
to cover all three retained (F_7) divisor blocks of projected degrees
(3,9,18)?

## Why this is now the strongest path

EXP-128 replaces the former 102-entry factor ledger by a five-block,
degree-75 squarefree CRT algebra. The section (h_7) already covers the
unique (F_3)-degree-15 and (F_6)-degree-30 blocks. Its only failures on
those curves project to the degree-9 and degree-18 blocks also seen in its
own (F_7) divisor. Thus a section that is a unit on the degree-30 (F_7)
ledger is the smallest exact target capable of closing every known finite
residual on the (AS\ne0) graph.

## Pre-run predictions

1. The shared degree-9 and degree-18 projected factors represent the same
   exact (X)-classes on their two curve descriptions, not merely equal
   (B)-projections.
2. At modular realizations of each of the three (F_7) blocks, the full
   augmented matrix has rank 125; hence the residual is a zero of the chosen
   section, not a rank defect of the complete system.
3. Modular row selection finds at least one 125-row basis that is nonzero on
   probes from every realized CRT block and differs from the accepted
   (F_3,F_6,F_7) bases.
4. The selected basis admits exact reconstruction within the declared compute
   gate and yields a nonzero section in
   \(\mathbb Q(B)[X]/(F_7)\).
5. The section norm is coprime to the degree-30 (F_7) ledger. Equivalently,
   it is a unit in each of the three finite CRT blocks.

Predictions 1--3 form the selection gate. Predictions 4--5 are the exact
closure gate.

## Method

1. Reload and hash-check EXP-125, EXP-127, and EXP-128.
2. Recover exact (X)-classes on the shared blocks by quotient arithmetic.
3. Search deterministic finite-field probes satisfying (F_7=h_7=0), the
   graph equation, and (X=A^3). Record the represented CRT factor for every
   probe and compute full row-rank bases.
4. Greedily select a single row basis only after it is nonzero on all retained
   probes. If no such basis appears, retain a minimal multi-basis atlas instead
   of forcing a false single-section claim.
5. Reconstruct each accepted basis exactly using the validated SCC/block
   determinant method, restrict it to (F_7), and test its norm against the
   three ledger factors using exact gcds and Bezout identities.

## Redirect rules

- If the shared projected factors have different (X)-classes, keep them as
  distinct geometric blocks and correct the union count.
- If a complete-system rank defect appears, stop minor selection and analyze
  that block as a possible inconsistency of the restricted family.
- If one basis cannot cover all blocks but a finite atlas can, persist the
  atlas and test the ideal generated by its norms in the CRT algebra.
- If exact reconstruction is too expensive, persist the modular certificate
  only as reconnaissance; do not promote it to a characteristic-zero result.

## Scope

This experiment concerns only the retained finite divisor on the (AS\ne0)
EXP-123 graph. Even a full PASS does not address (V(R,S)), (A=0), the
full four-parameter restriction, the complete ((72,108)) system, the degree
floor, or JC(2).
