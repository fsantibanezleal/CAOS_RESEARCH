# EXP-113 - Stratify the 36-core by parameter groups and cycle support

## Question

Can the exact 36-column cyclic core from EXP-112 be reduced further before
computing determinants, by separating the forced direction and identifying a
small parameter subset that carries its strong connectivity?

## Motivation

EXP-112 reduces the selected 125-by-125 full-family minor to one 36-by-36
block depending on 24 parameters, plus three forced one-dimensional factors.
The 24 directions split naturally by \(x\)-degree:
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_0&=\{(0,j):1\leq j\leq7\},\\
G_1&=\{(1,j):0\leq j\leq8\},\\
G_2&=\{(2,j):2\leq j\leq9\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
EXP-100 identifies \((0,1),(1,7)\) as the first genuine interior cycle after
forced normalization, but the full 36-core may require more directions for
strong connectivity.

Before any 24-variable determinant expansion, the exact cycle support should
be stratified.

## Premise dependencies

- [MV] EXP-112 supplies the exact pinned basis, 36-column component, and
  24 active parameter directions.
- [MV] EXP-100 supplies the first non-forced two-direction cycle.
- [D] If a parameter subset has a smaller SCC decomposition, determinants on
  that stratum factor through those blocks.
- [D] A deletion-minimal parameter set that keeps the full 36 vertices
  strongly connected is a rigorous upper bound on the number of directions
  needed to generate the full graph complexity. It is not guaranteed
  cardinality-minimal.

## Predictions

1. [MV] The forced direction alone has the known 13-dimensional nonzero
   eigenspace on the 36-core.
2. [C] Removing \((1,0)\) splits the 36-core into smaller strongly connected
   components.
3. [C] At least one single \(x\)-degree group has a proper SCC decomposition.
4. [C] A deletion-minimal full-connectivity support uses substantially fewer
   than all 24 directions.
5. [MV] The pair \((0,1),(1,7)\) remains cyclic but does not alone make all 36
   vertices strongly connected.

## Method

- Reconstruct the exact EXP-112 pinned basis and 36-core matrices.
- Compute SCC profiles for:
  - the forced direction alone;
  - all directions except the forced direction;
  - each \(G_i\);
  - each pair \(G_i\cup G_j\);
  - the known pair \((0,1),(1,7)\).
- Starting from all 24 directions, remove directions deterministically whenever
  full 36-vertex strong connectivity survives. Repeat to a deletion-minimal
  support.
- Repeat the deletion pass in reverse order as an adversarial control.
- Persist both supports, every exact SCC profile, and the direction-interaction
  traces \(\operatorname{tr}(N_iN_j)\).

## One-sidedness

- A PASS that splits the core proves an exact block factorization on the
  corresponding parameter stratum.
- A small deletion-minimal support proves that full graph complexity can
  already be generated by that explicit subset; it does not prove the other
  parameters are absent from the determinant.
- Failure to split after removing the forced direction refutes the proposed
  second compression by this invariant. It does not block determinant charts
  or alternative row bases.

## Adversarial validation

- All graph edges and traces are exact over \(\mathbb Q\).
- Forward and reverse deletion orders must both produce full-connectivity
  supports and are reported separately.
- The EXP-100 pair is a positive cycle control and a negative full-connectivity
  control.

## Invariant-first note

SCC size and trace interactions are cheaper than symbolic determinants. They
identify whether the 24-variable core is genuinely coupled or only appears so
because the forced direction was included.

## Compute budget and kill criterion

CPU-only exact run, expected under one minute, budget three minutes. Stop and
record an inconclusive result if exact normalization or trace computation
exceeds the budget.
