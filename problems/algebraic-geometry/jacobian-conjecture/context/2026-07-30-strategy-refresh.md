# Jacobian strategy refresh: curve divisors and current primary sources

Date: 2026-07-30.

This refresh was completed before declaring EXP-126. It asks whether the
recursive graph atlas remains the strongest exact route after EXP-125, and
whether a different mathematical viewpoint changes the next experiment.

## Primary-source sweep

The following current or load-bearing primary sources were checked against
the persisted GGHV applicability record.

1. Guccione, Guccione, Horruitiner, and Valqui, *Increasing the degree of a
   possible counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture from 100 to 108*,
   arXiv:2204.14178. The paper leaves the \((72,108)\) pair and supplies the
   reduced polynomial systems used by this program:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14178>.
2. Lee and Li, *On the two-dimensional Jacobian conjecture: Magnus' formula
   revisited, IV*, arXiv:2408.01279. Its proved inner-vertex region and its
   conjectural generator program apply to the original Keller pair:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.01279>.
3. Makar-Limanov and Trakhtenberg, *Properties of a Jacobian mate*, MPIM
   Preprint 2024 (33), now published in 2026. It derives restrictions by
   Newton resolution of an original Jacobian mate:
   <https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/5148/>.
4. Makar-Limanov, *On the Newton polytope of a Jacobian pair*,
   arXiv:2106.06869. It constrains the Newton polytope and geometric degree
   of a minimal original Jacobian pair:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06869>.
5. Jelonek, *On mappings with Jacobian one*, arXiv:2607.20597. Theorem 2.2
   organizes each irreducible component of the bounded-degree
   constant-Jacobian parameter space into an automorphism component or a
   generically counterexample component:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20597>.
6. Nguyen Van Chau, *Non-proper value set and the Jacobian condition*,
   arXiv:math/0305088. A nonsingular non-proper polynomial map of
   \(\mathbb C^2\), if one exists, has a non-proper value curve with one point
   at infinity:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0305088>.

## Applicability audit

The fresh sweep changes no premise already decided by EXP-095 through
EXP-097.

- The Newton and inner-polynomial results constrain the hypothetical original
  Keller pair. EXP-095/096 instantiated their present proved consequences,
  while EXP-097 showed that an absolute resultant-degree condition is not a
  typed equation on the final reduced coefficients after localization and
  inversion. They remain reconstruction filters, not direct equations on the
  51-parameter GGHV system.
- Jelonek's component theorem is valuable global framing, but the current
  graph factors are components of a determinantal exceptional locus inside a
  reduced bracket-\(x^2\) coefficient family. No proved map identifies them
  with components of the bounded-degree constant-Jacobian parameter space.
- The non-proper-value-curve route applies to an original nonsingular
  polynomial map. It does not directly type a condition on the reduced
  Laurent pair without the same missing boundary ledger.

No source found in this sweep closes the \(F_6\) or \(F_7\) strata, supersedes
the GGHV reduction, or supplies the missing transport map. Heavy work on any
of these global routes remains gated on an explicit applicability bridge.

## New viewpoint: minors as sections on residual curves

Let \(\mathcal M(A,B,C)\) be the complete normalized augmented matrix and let
\(I_{125}(\mathcal M)\) be its maximal-minor ideal. The constructible atlas is
the open cover generated by selected maximal minors. EXP-123/124 reduce one
part of the common zero locus to the graph
\[
R(X,B)+Y S(X,B)=0,\qquad X=A^3,\quad Y=A^2C,
\]
and then to the factor curves \(F_3F_6F_7=0\).

On the principal open \(AS\ne0\), graph substitution turns another normalized
minor into a rational section whose zero set is represented by an exact
numerator \(H(X,B)\). On
\[
C_i=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q[X,B]/(F_i),
\]
the class \(h_i=H\bmod F_i\) has three decisive possibilities:

1. \(h_i=0\): the selected chart vanishes identically and the basis is
   refuted for this component.
2. \(h_i\) is a unit on the required open: the component is closed there.
3. \(h_i\ne0\) but is not a unit: its zero divisor is finite on the affine
   curve after boundary points are separated.

For \(F_6\), which is quadratic in \(X\), write the quotient remainder as
\[
h_6=U(B)X+V(B).
\]
The exact function-field norm
\[
\operatorname{Norm}(h_6)
 =\operatorname{Res}_X(F_6,U(B)X+V(B))
\]
is then a univariate polynomial in \(B\). A nonzero norm proves that the new
minor covers a dense open of \(C_6\), and its roots contain the finite
recursive residual. Direct quotient multiplication gives an independent
norm check and avoids relying on a generic Groebner basis.

This is simultaneously:

- a commutative-algebra view through the maximal-minor/Fitting locus;
- a curve/function-field view through quotient rings and norms;
- a divisor view in which every new chart removes a dense open and leaves a
  finite, auditable zero divisor;
- an algorithmic view that replaces ambient elimination by degree-two
  arithmetic over \(\mathbb Q(B)\).

## Strategy decision

The recursive graph atlas remains P0 because it is the only current route
that both applies to the actual complete GGHV matrix and has reduced
dimension exactly in every accepted round since EXP-112. EXP-126 is
therefore retained, but reframed as a determinantal-divisor computation on
\(C_6\).

The route will be redirected only if the selected \(F_6\) basis vanishes
identically, its cyclic block exceeds the declared exact budget, or the norm
fails to reduce the residual to finite dimension. In that event, the next
choice is another persisted \(F_6\) basis or a multi-minor quotient gcd, not
ambient Groebner elimination.

No result in this refresh validates or invalidates \((72,108)\) or \(JC(2)\).
