# An independent exact verification of the 2026 counterexample to Goemans' unsplittable-flow cost conjecture, with the violation constant it forces

Current public version: v0.03, published 2026-09-18 at
[10.5281/zenodo.22835200](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22835200), under concept DOI
[10.5281/zenodo.21554258](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21554258).

The paper reports the exact rational verification of the seven-vertex counterexample announced in July 2026 (EXP-001,
EXP-002), the violation constant 16/15 it forces, the survival of every proved result on the instance, the class
boundary (a K4 subdivision, one structure past series-parallel), the exact separation linear program with the optimality
of the published cost vector (EXP-003), and the single-terminal theorem with the two-terminal necessary condition.

A revision is in preparation (backlog UFB-066): the current lower bounds of S. Nikolenko and A. De la Fuente with their
credit, the frontier value (EXP-004), the exact checks of the Muffatti and odd-cycle families (EXP-005, EXP-006) and the
independent re-check of the record instances (EXP-007).
