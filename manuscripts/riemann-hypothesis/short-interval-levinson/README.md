# Levinson's method in short intervals and simple zeros of the zeta function

Version `v0.01`, dated 26 September 2026. Not yet deposited.

The manuscript localizes Levinson's method, with Conrey's general operator
polynomial `Q`, to the intervals `(T,T+T^theta]` for mollifier exponents
`nu<theta-1/2`, and shows that on such windows it counts distinct sign changes
of Hardy's function with density `kappa=1-log(c)/R`. Degree-201 operator
polynomials certify `kappa>0.7170 nu`. Through the Hilbert-parity inequality
of the companion short-interval paper, every fixed `theta` in `[0.534,1)` has
a positive proportion of simple critical zeros in `(T,T+T^theta]`; at
`theta=0.5459` the lower density exceeds `0.0177638`.

The onset uses Wang's short-interval pair theorem (arXiv:2609.07918v1, a
recent unreviewed preprint). The result is asymptotic and does not prove the
Riemann hypothesis.
