# A sharp three-point kernel bound and conditional proportions of zeta zeros

Version `v0.02`, dated 2 November 2026 (correction of `v0.01`, 24 September 2026).

The manuscript proves the sharp auxiliary ratio bound `R(alpha,beta)<=sqrt(2)`
in Wang's three-point kernel argument, with equality exactly at `(0,1)` and
`(1,0)`. This theorem and the kernel-level inequality are claimed
unconditionally.

Substitution into Wang's global framework (arXiv:2609.24167v1) gives the
simple-critical proportion

`0.6725007995946757558283550562963947...`

and the distinct-zero proportion

`0.8362503997973378779141775281481973...`.

On 6 October 2026 the author replaced arXiv:2609.24167 by a notice that a new
method is needed. These proportions and the short-interval corollary are
therefore conditional on an argument its author no longer offers and are not
claimed. The short-interval companion also depends on the attributed rank-six
input used in EXP-008. Wang's local pair theorem (arXiv:2609.07918) is
unchanged in its v2. The result is asymptotic and does not prove the Riemann
hypothesis.
