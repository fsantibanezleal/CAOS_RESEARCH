# A sharp three-point kernel bound and improved proportions of zeta zeros

Version `v0.01`, dated 24 September 2026.

The manuscript proves the sharp auxiliary ratio bound `R(alpha,beta)<=sqrt(2)`
in Wang's three-point kernel argument, with equality exactly at `(0,1)` and
`(1,0)`.  Substitution into the source-pinned global framework gives the
certified simple-critical proportion

`0.6725007995946757558283550562963947...`

and the distinct-zero proportion

`0.8362503997973378779141775281481973...`.

The global transfer depends on Wang arXiv:2609.24167v1, a recent unreviewed
preprint.  The short-interval companion also depends on the attributed
rank-six input used in EXP-008.  The result is asymptotic and does not prove
the Riemann hypothesis.
