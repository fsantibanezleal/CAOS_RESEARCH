## Novelty, splitting, and non-claims

Classical potential, complement and linear-extension methods are not claimed
as new general techniques. The contribution is the explicit, complete
application to this coefficient presentation and the proved quadratic family
of valuation-one two-torsion summands. The Zenodo description explicitly
attributes the prior public counterexample to Son Pham. The manuscript
disclaims discovery priority and cites the earlier CAOS family record by
immutable DOI; it does not provide a separate history of the original discovery.

The theorem does not determine the full cokernel, its free rank, odd torsion,
remaining two-primary factors, any complete quotient upper bound, or the
lower-strand recurrence. Coincidence with earlier isolated ranks 3,4,5,7 does
not identify that isolated object with this full presentation. The separate
relative-completion quotient likewise needs actual compatible maps.

The 53-page main paper already has a different organizing purpose; adding
this self-contained source/dual theory there would obscure both narratives.
A complementary manuscript is therefore appropriate. Existing main v0.23
and curvilinear v0.02 PDFs are unchanged. Their hypotheses are not invalidated
by the EXP-054 correction or these new theorems, so no erratum is required.

This audit authorizes only the mathematical scope. Stabilized build, all-page
render review, sole-author metadata, exact upload and fresh unauthenticated
public-file/latest-version verification are recorded separately.
