## New validated material

EXP-058 proves a local rational obstruction at p=8 with an independently checked
40-row integer separating functional. It does not exclude full-domain sources.

EXP-059 proves a complete integral potential basis for the D-kernel sector with
fixed exterior high set `{6p,8p-4}`, for every p>=8. Its rank is `binom(p-1,2)`;
each unit basis chain has height one, at most `3p-5` terms and at most seven K
rows in its complete original boundary. This is a genuine uniform sector theorem,
not a finite-table inference. The separate 861-chain audit verifies its formulas;
the reconstruction proof establishes integral completeness.

## Publication decision and novelty boundary

This is substantial material for the proposed connecting-parity companion, but
does not yet meet the existing stronger publication gate. The basis is complete
for one D-kernel sector, not for the full connecting quotient. It does not yet
give a nonzero torsion class, a complete critical complex, a recurrence, or a
demonstrated independently transferable homological consequence. The classical
potential, complement and unit-minor techniques are not themselves claimed novel.

Do not require resolution of the entire research program before publication:
a proved uniform nonzero torsion class, a complete finite critical complex,
or a comparably strong cross-sector consequence could independently justify a
new companion. First freeze and independently validate the needed cross-high
relations, including all C0 corrections and exceptional high-free faces.

Neither new experiment changes a premise of the frozen main manuscript (which
ends at EXP-036) or its curvilinear companion (EXP-026). No erratum applies.
Accordingly no PDF was changed, no new Zenodo version reserved, and no upload or
publication was performed for EXP-058/059. The public version/hash checks from
earlier on 2026-09-05 UTC remain explicitly dated; this note is not a new download.
