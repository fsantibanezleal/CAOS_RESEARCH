Self-questioning (methodology 11, item 2). Before the dossier the coordinating session ranked this problem low: a
statement over every smooth projective variety with no reduction. The dossier keeps that ranking and adds three facts
the earlier review did not have.

   statement: a class that is Hodge on every fibre of a family of abelian varieties and algebraic on one fibre with

3. The corrections to this repository's review of 2026-11-22 (Section "Summary of the dossier", item 3): the
   250,000-line figure belongs to another statement, and the vacuity of one public Lean statement of the conjecture
   is on record.

Consequence for the choice. Abelian varieties are one family; the prize statement is for every smooth projective
variety and nothing reduces it to a family. The steps that can be checked in days are computations on Fermat
varieties and audits of formal statements. The problem is not chosen as the target (Section 11).

## 1. The prize statement

### 1.1 Exact statement (official problem description)

Source [P]: Pierre Deligne, "The Hodge Conjecture", Clay Mathematics Institute official problem description. URL https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/hodge.pdf, read 2026-11-24, archived at `src/hodge_clay.pdf` (5 pages), SHA-256 `e308d945ea3cf5dad8b187a06509013712c467b589039eb41b365cc4c988f0c8`; extracted text `src/hodge_clay.txt`.

Definition quoted [P]: "Rational ( p, p)-classes are called Hodge classes. They form the group H 2p(X, Q) ∩ H p,p(X) = H 2p(X, Q) ∩ F p ⊂ H 2p(X, C)."

The prize statement [P]: "Hodge Conjecture. On a projective non-singular algebraic variety over C, any Hodge class is a rational linear combination of classes cl(Z) of algebraic cycles."

In symbols: for every smooth projective complex variety X and every p with 0 <= p <= dim X, the cycle class map cl_p : CH^p(X) tensor Q to H^{2p}(X,Q) has image equal to Hdg^p(X) := H^{2p}(X,Q) ∩ H^{p,p}(X).

Remarks in the same document that fix what is and is not included [P]:
- "(i) By Chow's theorem, on a complex projective variety, algebraic cycles are the same as closed analytic subspaces."
- The conjecture "cannot hold integrally": "the Hodge conjecture cannot hold integrally" (Atiyah-Hirzebruch, remark (iv)); "(v) The assumption in the Hodge conjecture that X be algebraic cannot be weakened to X being merely Kahler. See Zucker's appendix to [11] for counterexamples where X is a complex torus."
- Codimension one is Kodaira-Spencer via the exponential sequence (remark (iii)).
- Clay's front page for the problem [P, https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/, read 2026-11-24]: "The Hodge conjecture is known in certain special cases, e.g., when the solution set has dimension less than four. But in dimension four it is unknown."

### 1.2 What counts as a solution under Clay's rules

Source [P]: Clay "Rules for the Millennium Prize Problems", PDF https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/millennium_prize_rules_0.pdf, read 2026-11-24, archived `src/clay_rules.pdf`, SHA-256 `9b5003745c0ae7268dc7769f83e1c61eaca674f28631f1df2cd8400976640b2a`. The HTML page https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/rules/ (archived `src/clay_rules.html`) states: "(i) the proposed solution must be published in a Qualifying Outlet (see §6), and (ii) at least two years must have passed since publication, and (iii) the proposed solution must have received general acceptance in the global mathematics community".

Rules text (PDF section 4) [P]: a prize may be awarded only if "a. the Proposed Solution has been published by a Qualifying Outlet ... b. at least two (2) years have elapsed since publication ... c. the Proposed Solution has achieved general acceptance in the global mathematics community ... d. the Proposed Solution has satisfactorily answered the questions raised by the Problem's official description". Section 5(a): "Only a complete mathematical solution to a Problem as it is defined in its official Problem description ... will be eligible". Section 5(d): "A paper that does not address or refer to the specific mathematical questions set out in detail in the official Problem description will not be considered to be a Potential Solution of one of the Problems, even if it addresses closely related scientific questions."

Counterexample, section 5(c) [P]: "In the case of the other Problems, if a counterexample is proposed, such counterexample shall be evaluated using the same evaluation procedure as if it were a Proposed Solution. i. If, in the opinion of CMI, the counterexample effectively resolves the Problem, then CMI may recommend the award of a Prize. ii. If, alternatively, the counterexample shows that the original Problem survives after reformulation or elimination of some special case, then CMI may recommend that a small prize ... be awarded". So a counterexample to the rational conjecture (for example a Hodge class on a complex projective variety that is not algebraic) is eligible; a counterexample to the integral or Kahler versions is not a solution (those failures are already in the official text).

Consequence for 2026 claims: a theorem for a subclass (CM abelian varieties, Kummer type, Fermat fourfolds) is not a Proposed Solution; at best it earns citation in a later solution (section 8(b) of the rules: Clay "will pay special attention to the question of whether a Prize solution depends crucially on insights published prior").

### 1.3 Neighbouring statements that are NOT the prize statement

Each is a neighbour, a corollary, or a weakened or strengthened form; none settles the prize (Section 5(d) of the rules).
1. Hodge conjecture for a class of varieties: abelian varieties; CM abelian varieties; Weil-type abelian fourfolds and sixfolds; K3 surfaces and powers; hyper-Kahler varieties of a deformation type; Fermat varieties of given degree and dimension; ball quotients and Shimura varieties; moduli spaces of curves (survey by Payne, arXiv:2605.20453) [P for the abstracts, Section 2].

5. Variational Hodge conjecture (Grothendieck) and its p-adic and formal versions (Morrow, arXiv:1408.6783; Bloch-Esnault-Kerz, arXiv:1310.1773) [P abstracts].
6. Tate conjecture (Clay's text: "Those conjectures are open even for H 2" [P]) and its proved cases; Mumford-Tate conjecture; Hodge-Tate conjugacy.
7. Standard conjectures of Grothendieck (Lefschetz type, Hodge type, Kunneth) [K].
8. Absolute Hodge classes and motivated classes (known for abelian varieties: Deligne, Andre; Clay PDF section 6 [P]).
9. Milne's rationality conjecture (the question at the end of Clay section 6: "Is kappa a rational number?") is a weakened consequence of Hodge, not the Hodge conjecture [P].

## 3. Anatomy of the strongest approaches

Format per approach: proves today; where it stops; named barrier; references. Labels: [P] abstract or text opened today; [K] classical knowledge.

A1. Lefschetz (1,1) by the exponential sequence (Kodaira-Spencer).
- Proves: every integral (1,1)-class is c_1 of a line bundle, hence the conjecture in codimension 1 and by hard Lefschetz in codimension n-1; this gives all cases with dim X <= 3 [P Clay PDF remark (iii), front page "less than four"; K for the hard Lefschetz reduction].
- Stops: no analogue of H^{0,2}(X)=0 as an obstruction group exists in higher codimension; the exponential sequence does not iterate to cycles of codimension p >= 2 because holomorphic vector bundle data replaces line bundles and brings in Chern classes of bundles [P Clay remark (ii), (iv)].
- Barrier: absence of a construction of cycles from cohomology classes ("Progress is blocked by a lack of methods to construct interesting algebraic cycles" [P Clay section 5]).

A2. Induction on dimension through Lefschetz pencils and intermediate Jacobians (Griffiths, Zucker).
- Proves: cubic fourfolds (Zucker 1977 [P cited in Clay PDF reference [11]]).
- Stops: needs "all of Jp(H) comes from algebraic cycles", but "Cases are known where Ap(X)/A0p(X) is of infinite rank" [P Clay section 3, citing Voisin on the Griffiths group of a general Calabi-Yau threefold].
- Barrier: infinite generation of the Griffiths group; no conjecture predicts which subgroup of J^p(X) is algebraic [P].

A3. Algebraic vector bundles and K-theory (integral version).
- Proves: a necessary condition for effective integral classes (the Atiyah-Hirzebruch differentials d_r must kill a class); counterexamples to the integral version [P Clay remark (iv)]; Benoist-Ottem (Enriques surface times a very general curve of genus >= 1, "does not satisfy the integral Hodge conjecture for 1-cycles", arXiv:1802.01845 [P]); Engel-de Gaay Fortman-Schreieder ("the cohomology class of any curve on a very general principally polarized abelian variety of dimension at least 4 is an even multiple of the minimal class", arXiv:2507.15704 [P]); positive integral results: Totaro for threefolds of Kodaira dimension zero with H^0(K) nonzero (arXiv:1907.08670 [P]); Perry for two-dimensional Calabi-Yau categories (arXiv:2004.03163 [P]).
- Stops: integral classes only; rational conjecture unaffected.
- Barrier: torsion and the topological obstruction (cobordism, Totaro [K]); what survives rationally is not controlled by K-theory ("One has no idea of which classes should be effective" [P Clay]).

A4. Kahler version and non-algebraic arguments.
- Proves: failure for complex tori (Zucker, [P Clay remark (v)]) and Voisin's counterexample to the Kahler extension (IMRN 2002) [K, not opened].
- Stops: any proof must use projectivity in a way Kahler geometry does not.
- Barrier: the algebraicity input; analytic (Hodge-theoretic, heat-kernel, current-theoretic) arguments that work for all compact Kahler manifolds cannot prove the conjecture.

A5. Absolute Hodge classes (Deligne), motivated classes (Andre), reduction to Weil classes.
- Proves: on abelian varieties every Hodge class is absolute Hodge [P Clay section 6: "On abelian varieties, Hodge classes at least share many properties of cohomology classes of algebraic cycles: they are 'absolutely Hodge' [3], even 'motivated' [1]"]; for CM abelian varieties Hodge classes are expressed by divisor classes and split Weil classes (Deligne, Andre; Milne arXiv:2010.08857: "the Hodge classes on abelian varieties of CM-type can be expressed in terms of divisor classes and split Weil classes" [P]).
- Stops: gives algebraic relations among periods and quasi-periods, "but does not allow for reduction modulo p" [P Clay section 6]; does not produce cycles.
- Barrier: absolute Hodge is strictly weaker than algebraic, and no technique is known to pass from one to the other except by explicit constructions.

A6. Algebraic geometry of the Hodge locus: Cattani-Deligne-Kaplan; o-minimal proof; typical and atypical loci.
- Proves: the locus where a class becomes Hodge is a countable union of algebraic subvarieties [P Clay section 4, citing CDK]; "any pure polarized variation of integral Hodge structures ... definable ... we recover that the Hodge locus of (S, V) is a countable union of algebraic subvarieties" (Bakker-Klingler-Tsimerman, arXiv:1810.04801 [P]); Ax-Schanuel for variations of Hodge structure (Bakker-Tsimerman, arXiv:1712.05088 [P]); "if the level of V is at least 3, this Hodge locus is in fact a finite union of such special subvarieties" for the factorwise positive period dimension part (Baldi-Klingler-Ullmo, arXiv:2107.08838 [P]); quasi-projectivity of period images (arXiv:1811.12230 [P]); finiteness for self-dual classes (Bakker-Grimm-Schnell-Tsimerman, arXiv:2112.06995 [P]).
- Stops: they prove algebraicity of the locus of classes, not algebraicity of the class by a cycle; the Clay text: "The Hodge conjecture implies that the locus ... is a denumerable union of algebraic subvarieties of S (known: see [4]), and is defined over Q-bar (unknown)" [P]. The field-of-definition statement is open.
- Barrier: definability gives algebraic structure of loci; it gives no cycle.

A7. Variational Hodge conjecture and deformation of cycles (Grothendieck; Bloch-Esnault-Kerz; Morrow; semiregularity).
- Proves: "a formal deformation problem for rational algebraic cycle classes ... a close connection between the existence of a Chow-Kunneth decomposition and the existence of expected deformations of cycles. This observation applies in particular to abelian schemes" (BEK, arXiv:1310.1773 [P]); the crystalline analogue "We prove the conjecture for divisors, and an infinitesimal variant of the conjecture for cycles of higher codimension" (Morrow, arXiv:1408.6783 [P]); Markman uses semiregularity (Buchweitz-Flenner) for the Weil family [P abstract, arXiv:2502.03415]; the Voisin post says semiregularity probably will not suffice in general [S].
- Stops: divisors and infinitesimal orders.
- Barrier: obstruction spaces of the Hilbert scheme and formal-to-algebraic passage; the Q_p-coefficient version fails for line bundles (Lazda-Pal, arXiv:1701.05017 per a search snippet, NOT OPENED).

A8. Weil classes and abelian varieties of small dimension.
- Proves: Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties of dimension <= 3 and simple prime-dimensional ones [K: Tankeev, Ribet; not opened]; Schoen for abelian fourfolds of Weil type with discriminant 1 under conditions [P, quoted text of arXiv:2504.13607 line 61-63 mentions Schoen's sporadic cases]; Markman: "We prove that the Weil classes are algebraic for all abelian sixfold of Weil type of discriminant -1, for all imaginary quadratic number fields. The algebraicity of the Weil classes follows for all abelian fourfolds of Weil type ... by a degeneration argument of C. Schoen" (arXiv:2502.03415 [P]); Floccari-Fu and van Geemen-Rapagnetta give new proofs for discriminant 1 fourfolds (rows 7 and 8).
- Stops: "Markman's theorem requires discriminant -1" (McMullen's abstract names the obstructions: "CM isolation, absence of a K-secant structure, and uncontrolled discriminant" [P]); sixfolds with other discriminants and eightfolds are open apart from the OpenAI claims.
- Barrier: constructing the secant sheaf or hyper-Kahler partner needs a polarization of discriminant (-1)^n type; outside it there is no construction.

A9. Hyper-Kahler geometry (K3^[n], generalized Kummer, OG6), Kuga-Satake.
- Proves: the Floccari and Floccari-Fu results (rows 5 to 7) [P]; Madapusi Pera: the Kuga-Satake construction gives an open immersion of moduli of K3 surfaces into an orthogonal Shimura variety, used for the Tate conjecture for K3 surfaces in odd characteristic (arXiv:1301.6326 [P]).
- Stops: n+1 prime for Kummer type; the general case conditional on the Lefschetz standard conjecture for the variety [P Floccari-Fu].
- Barrier: algebraicity of the Kuga-Satake correspondence for general K3 surfaces is open (the withdrawn OpenAI claim and the conditional OpenAI paper both reduce to it [P, README and CONTENTS]).

A10. Arithmetic quotients and special cycles (Kudla-Millson; Bergeron-Millson-Moeglin; Bergeron-Li-Millson-Moeglin).
- Proves: for a closed ball quotient S of dimension n, Hodge classes are algebraic "for all degree k away from the neighborhood ]n/3, 2n/3[ of the middle degree" (arXiv:1306.1515 [P], conditional on stabilization of a twisted trace formula as stated in the abstract); the Noether-Lefschetz conjecture for K3 moduli, "low degree cohomology classes of arithmetic manifolds of orthogonal type are dual to the classes of special cycles" (arXiv:1412.3774 [P]).
- Stops: the middle range; the proofs use endoscopic classification of automorphic representations (Arthur, Mok) [P abstract].
- Barrier: middle degree of Shimura varieties is where theta lifts to cohomology classes of special cycles fail to exhaust the Hodge classes; this is exactly the range the OpenAI CM proof attacks through compact ball quotients of signature (2,1) [P cm_paper.txt Section 4 and 1.3].

A11. Tate conjecture and the finite-field side.
- Proves: Tate for K3 surfaces in odd characteristic (Madapusi Pera, arXiv:1301.6326 [P]), characteristic 2 and finite fields (Charles, Maulik; [K], not opened); Tate for divisors on abelian varieties over finite fields (Tate, Honda) [K]; the standard conjecture of Hodge type for powers of abelian threefolds (Agugliaro, arXiv:2510.21562 [P]).
- Stops: higher codimension on abelian varieties over finite fields. The OpenAI CM paper claims this follows from its theorem through Milne ("Two theorems of Milne then give the Tate conjecture for all abelian varieties over finite fields" [P cm_paper.txt, Introduction]); so under the claim only is it settled.
- Barrier: Frobenius eigenvalue (Honda-Tate) data give classes in l-adic cohomology but no cycles; no reduction to Hodge without the CM reduction.

A12. Tropical and combinatorial Hodge theory.
- Proves: Hard Lefschetz, Hodge-Riemann and monodromy-weight for smooth projective tropical varieties (Amini-Piquerez, arXiv:2007.07826 [P]); "the tropical Hodge conjecture for smooth projective tropical varieties which are rationally triangulable" (arXiv:2012.13142 [P]); Adiprasito-Huh-Katz Hodge theory for matroids (arXiv:1511.02888 [P]); a tropical counterexample to the positive-current version (Babaee-Huh, arXiv:1502.00299 [P]); tropically motivated disproof of the integral version (arXiv:2507.15704 [P]).
- Stops: the tropical conjecture without the triangulability assumption; the transfer from tropical to complex Hodge classes (a counterexample would need the tropical Hodge conjecture to fail for a tropical abelian variety, and Brosnan says the degeneration needed is only available at discriminant (-1)^n [P]).
- Barrier: lack of a theorem that algebraic tropical cycles lift to complex cycles in families (cohomological tropicality: Aksnes-Amini-Piquerez-Shaw, arXiv:2307.02945 [P]).

A13. Standard conjectures.
- Proves: Lefschetz-type standard conjecture would give the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties (Milne's abstract: "Grothendieck's standard conjecture of Lefschetz type implies the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties (Abdulali, Andre, ...)" arXiv:2010.08857 [P]; which Lefschetz-type conjecture is meant is UNVERIFIED, I read the abstract only); Floccari-Fu, same implication for generalized Kummer type [P].
- Stops: the standard conjectures are open beyond abelian varieties, curves, surfaces and some others [K].
- Barrier: the algebraic cycle Lambda (the inverse of the Lefschetz operator) is not constructed in general.

A14. Fermat varieties and explicit linear cycles.
- Proves: the Hodge conjecture for Fermat varieties in a range (Shioda; Aoki) [K, not opened]; for Fermat fourfolds of odd degree at most 199 (Jumagulov [P]); periods of linear cycles in even-dimensional Fermat varieties and the variational Hodge conjecture for cycles obtained by monodromy of linear cycles (Movasati-Villaflor Loyola, arXiv:1705.00084 [P]).
- Stops: the non-decomposable, non-standard classes at m = 33 etc.; "Seven of the thirteen are gap classes outside Aoki's standard lattice calculus" [P Jumagulov]; "an exact Jacobi-sum computation at p = 67 shows that no cycle defined over Q(33) projects nontrivially onto the exceptional m = 33 block" [P].
- Barrier: Fermat varieties are CM-like (Jacobi-sum characters) but their Hodge classes are not all produced by products of curves; the generic degree needs a uniform cycle construction.

## 4. Solved analogues and model cases

For each: where the question is settled, the mechanism, what transfers, what provably does not.

M1. Degree one (Lefschetz (1,1)). Settled for every smooth projective complex variety [P Clay]. Mechanism: exponential sequence plus H^2(X,O) vanishing of the (0,2) part; equivalently the first Chern class of a line bundle. Transfers: the integral statement in codimension 1; the passage to H^{2n-2} by hard Lefschetz [K]. Does not transfer: higher codimension needs vector bundles, where the integral version fails [P Clay remark (iv)].

M2. Low dimension. dim <= 3 by M1 and hard Lefschetz [P front page]; dim 4 open in general, known for abelian fourfolds (Markman, Moonen-Zarhin, and the new proofs of rows 5 to 8) and for cubic fourfolds (Zucker) [P]. Mechanism: reduction of the conjecture to codimension 2 in dimension 4; the abelian case uses Weil classes. What transfers: the Weil class as the test object. What does not: no construction for general fourfolds.

M3. Special families with explicit cycles. Fermat varieties (Shioda, Aoki; Jumagulov's machine census [P]); products of elliptic curves; diagonal complete intersections (OpenAI, "Abelian covers, Gale correspondences": a claim, row 4). Mechanism: Jacobi-sum characters and induction from curves (Shioda-Katsura inductive structure, [P quoted in Jumagulov's introduction]). Transfers to: CM abelian varieties and varieties covered by Fermat. Does not transfer: to families without a large automorphism group (the characters disappear).

M4. Ball quotients, Shimura varieties, special cycles outside the middle degrees (BMM [P]). Mechanism: theta lifts (Kudla-Millson) from smaller unitary or orthogonal groups to cohomology classes, plus the endoscopic classification to show the lifts exhaust the cohomology. What transfers: the machinery that the OpenAI CM proof reuses [P cm_paper.txt Section 1.3]. What does not: middle degree, and non-Shimura varieties.

M5. Absolute Hodge and motivated classes for abelian varieties (Deligne, Andre) [P Clay section 6]. Mechanism: Hodge classes on abelian varieties are fibres of absolute Hodge sections; Deligne's argument transports a Hodge class to every conjugate variety, and CM varieties play a central role (Hodge groups are tori) [P cm_paper.txt Section 1.1]. What transfers: reduction to CM and Weil-type classes. What does not: the passage from absolute Hodge to algebraic.

M6. Finite-field side. Tate conjecture for divisors on abelian varieties (Tate, Zarhin, Faltings) [K], for K3 surfaces (Madapusi Pera, Charles, Maulik; first one [P]). Mechanism: Kuga-Satake abelian variety attached to the K3 surface; Tate for endomorphisms of the Kuga-Satake variety [P abstract of arXiv:1301.6326]. What transfers: the Kuga-Satake bridge as a tool to turn divisors into endomorphisms. What does not: higher codimension for abelian varieties over finite fields (open, as the Clay text says "open even for H 2" for Tate in general [P]).

M7. Abelian varieties with small Mumford-Tate group. When the Hodge group is the full Sp or a unitary group of low rank, Hodge classes are generated by powers of the polarization or by Weil classes (Weil, Mumford, Tankeev, Ribet, Moonen-Zarhin) [K, not opened]. The OpenAI "Weil powers" abstract claims every self-power of an abelian variety of dimension at most five with an imaginary-quadratic action [P, claim]. What transfers: the reduction to Weil-type determinant classes. What does not: large dimension.

M8. Tropical setting. Settled for rationally triangulable smooth projective tropical varieties (Amini-Piquerez [P]). Mechanism: tropical Clemens-Schmid exact sequence. What transfers: the idea of testing Hodge by degeneration (Kontsevich). What does not: a lift of tropical cycles to complex cycles; Brosnan limits the degenerations available for Weil families [P].

M9. Absolute Hodge or motivated classes in the Hodge-to-Tate direction. Corollaries of the Hodge conjecture that are accessible: Milne's rationality question at the end of the Clay description [P section 6: "Is kappa a rational number? ..."]; the OpenAI Milne paper claims it for abelian varieties [P claim, row 4]. What transfers: nothing unconditional until row 1 is verified.

M10. Hyper-Kahler type families (K3^[n], Kummer, OG6). Settled in some deformation types and ranges [P rows 5 to 8]. Mechanism: for a hyper-Kahler X of known deformation type the cohomology is generated by a known subalgebra and the Kuga-Satake/Weil partners, and algebraicity of one class (such as the second Chern class of a sixfold [P row 8]) places the Weil class. What transfers: any family where a Hodge class on the abelian partner is the pullback of a natural algebraic class. What does not: no hyper-Kahler partner is known outside discriminant (-1)^n.

## 6. Candidate targets

Each is the prize statement or strictly on a route to it. Notation: for a smooth projective complex variety X, Hdg^p(X) := H^{2p}(X,Q) ∩ H^{p,p}(X), and cl^p : CH^p(X) tensor Q to Hdg^p(X).

T1. The prize statement. For every smooth projective X over C and every p, cl^p is surjective.
- Known: dimension <= 3; abelian fourfolds; many families (Sections 3 and 4). Why open: no uniform construction of cycles. Attacked by: all rows (1 to 18). Meaning: it is the prize.

T2. Hodge for abelian varieties, by the variational reduction. Let S be a connected component of a Hodge locus of the universal family of polarized abelian varieties with generic Mumford-Tate group G, f : A to S the family, and xi in Hdg^p(A_s) a class that is Hodge on every fibre (a flat Hodge section). Claim to prove: if xi is algebraic on one CM fibre A_{s_0}, then xi is algebraic on every fibre. 
- Known: Hodge classes are absolute Hodge [P Clay section 6]; CM fibres are Zariski dense in S [K, André-Oort style density; not opened]; the OpenAI claim covers every CM fibre (row 1, unverified); the Grothendieck variational conjecture is the general form [P Clay front matter in Section 4 and BEK abstract]. Why open: the variational Hodge conjecture itself is open beyond divisors and formal orders (Section 3, A7). Attacked by: rows 2, 5, 14, 3. Meaning: if T2 and the CM theorem hold, the Hodge conjecture holds for all abelian varieties; this is a statement strictly smaller than the prize and contains the unresolved case of Weil classes (Markman, McMullen).

T3. Weil classes on abelian sixfolds of general discriminant. Let K = Q(sqrt(-d)), A an abelian sixfold with K in End^0(A) acting on H^{1,0} with eigenvalue multiplicities (3,3), and W(A) := wedge^6_K H^1(A,Q) subset H^6(A,Q), a Q-vector space of dimension 2 consisting of classes of type (3,3). Claim: W(A) is in the image of cl^3 for every such A with discriminant not equal to -1.
- Known: discriminant -1 [P Markman arXiv:2502.03415]; discriminant 1 for fourfolds [P rows 7, 8]; CM members [P claim, unverified]. McMullen's abstract names the obstructions for the discriminant, CM isolation and absence of a K-secant structure [P]. Why open: no secant sheaf or hyper-Kahler partner outside discriminant (-1)^n. Attacked by: rows 1, 4, 6, 7, 14. Meaning: with T2 it settles the abelian case (given Deligne and Andre reduction via split Weil classes [P Milne abstract]).

T4. Hodge for generalized Kummer type in all dimensions. For every X deformation equivalent to Kum_n(A), of dimension 2n, and every m, cl^p is surjective on X^m. 
- Known: n + 1 prime [P Floccari-Fu]; n = 3 (sixfolds) [P Floccari 2308.02267]; dimension divisible by 4 under the Lefschetz standard conjecture for X [P]. Why open: the prime condition. Attacked by: rows 17, 1, 9. Meaning: gives hyper-Kahler cases and, through Floccari-Fu's methods, evidence on the Kuga-Satake bridge; not directly a step toward T1 but a fully new infinite family.

T5. Algebraicity of the Kuga-Satake correspondence for K3 surfaces. For S a projective K3 surface and KS(S) its Kuga-Satake abelian variety, Deligne's Hodge classes on S x KS(S) that realize the embedding of the transcendental lattice T(S) into H^2(KS(S), Q) (they are Hodge, indeed absolute Hodge [K, Deligne, not opened]) are in the image of cl on S x KS(S). The exact degree and normalization of the standard even-Clifford tensor are not fixed here (UNVERIFIED: not read in a source).
- Known: OpenAI's conditional paper: "algebraicity of one exact standard even-Clifford Kuga-Satake tensor implies both conjectures for every self-power" (CONTENTS.md line 773, a repository summary, [S]); the withdrawn paper tried to prove it; the Tate analogue is a theorem (Madapusi Pera [P]). Why open: the Betti Hodge class is only known absolute Hodge (Deligne). Attacked by: rows 1, 2, 14, 6. Meaning: Hodge for all powers of K3 surfaces, hence for all hyper-Kahler of K3^[n] type partially (Kuga-Satake connections).

T6. Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields in every codimension. For A over F_q, l != p, and p >= 0, the Q_l-span of Tate classes in H^{2p}(A_{F-bar}, Q_l(p)) (classes fixed by an open subgroup of Galois) is spanned by cycle classes.
- Known: divisors (Tate, Zarhin, Faltings, [K]); the claim that it follows from the CM theorem through Milne [P cm_paper.txt Section 8.2 and Introduction]. Why open: Clay "Those conjectures are open even for H 2" for the general Tate conjecture [P]. Attacked by: rows 2, 8, 16. Meaning: independent of Hodge for general varieties but equivalent in this case to a Hodge statement on CM abelian varieties, so a check that either implication fails would be informative about row 1.

T7. Milne's rationality conjecture (Clay's final question). For A/Q-bar with good reduction at w, A_0 its reduction, z in Hdg^p(A) a Hodge class, and D_1 ... D_p divisors on A_0 with p + (d-p) complementary dimension: the intersection number kappa(z, D) := <cl_l(z|A_0), D_1 ... D_{d-p}> lies in Q, and is independent of l and of the crystalline realization.
- Known: Clay poses it [P section 6]; the OpenAI paper claims all residue characteristics [P abstract]. Why open: independent confirmation missing. Attacked by: rows 16, 8, 14. Meaning: a consequence of the Hodge conjecture; a proof independent of Hodge would settle a corollary Clay itself isolates.

T8. The tropical Hodge conjecture for tropical abelian varieties without the triangulability assumption. For X a smooth projective tropical abelian variety, every rational class in H^{p,p}_trop (the (p,p) tropical homology, the tropical Hodge classes) is a rational combination of classes of tropical subvarieties (tropical cycles).
- Known: rationally triangulable case [P Amini-Piquerez arXiv:2012.13142]; Thomas's comment mentions an unchecked AI proof for tropical abelian varieties [S, comment only, NOT OPENED]. Why open: general case needs non-triangulable fans. Attacked by: row 7. Meaning: closes Kontsevich's counterexample route entirely; it does not prove the prize statement, it removes the only proposed counterexample route.

T9. Algebraicity of Fermat Hodge classes in all degrees. For X_m^4 in P^5 with every degree m, the Hodge (2,2) characters (a_0,...,a_5) in (Z/m)^6 with sum a_i = 0 and sum <t a_i> = 3m for all t in (Z/m)^* are algebraic.
- Known: odd m <= 199 [P Jumagulov]; classical cases m <= 20, primes, m = p^2 [P quoted from the introduction]. Why open: the family of closure criteria is finite; the m = 33 block has no cycle over Q(33) [P]. Attacked by: rows 12, 13, 3. Meaning: first non-CM-looking family at which a counterexample would be visible; a failure would be a refutation candidate (no failure expected; the computation is the test).
