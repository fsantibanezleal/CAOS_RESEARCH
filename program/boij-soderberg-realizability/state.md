12: the least d3 of a counterexample in characteristic 0 (10), the table over both rings, and the least realizable
multiple on 205 of the 220 rays; over the enveloping algebra the point (1,2,2,1) of (0, e, 3e, 4e) fails only in
characteristic 2 for 2 <= e <= 5, and the pure pairs form a family of dimension at least 4e - 4. After v0.02 (research records of 2026-11-20, EXP-009 to EXP-017 and eight reviewed derivations): for 2 <= e <= 10 and
for e = 12 the point fails only in characteristic 2 (a pure pair over GF(p) for every odd prime below 2e); in
characteristic p >= 3 it is realized for every e divisible by p or congruent to 1 modulo p, the realized e are closed
under multiplication by such d, and for even e it is realized over a finite field of characteristic p >= 2e - 1. The
linear family holds no pure member for p <= 2e - 5 (proved) and holds one for p = 2e - 3, 2e - 1 in the thirteen pairs
computed. Open: 15 rays with d3 >= 10; e = 11 at p = 3, 13, 17; e = 13 at p = 7, 11, 17, 19; e = 14 at p = 5, 11, 23;
