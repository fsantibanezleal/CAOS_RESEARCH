# tau-conjecture: lenses pass (2026-08-01, opening round)

Per methodology 10 (spine + at least two complementary lenses, multi-factorial
standing rule). What each lens means HERE and what it is expected to produce.

1. **Exclusion/obstruction (spine).** The census ladder: for each length
   $\tau$, exhaust the canonicalized constant-free SLPs and DECIDE
   $z_{\max}(\tau)$ exactly. Each completed length is a theorem-grade fact
   ("no program of length 5 has more than N distinct integer roots"),
   analogous to the per-degree exclusions of the Jacobian program. Limit:
   the ladder is unbounded; it cannot prove the conjecture, only map its
   bottom and hunt refutation mechanisms.

2. **Anatomy/construction.** The extremal witnesses at each length are the
   "known objects": reverse-engineer their mechanism (shifted-linear
   products, squaring towers, subtraction collisions), generalize to
   families, and measure/prove each family's asymptotic $z(\tau)$ rate. A
   superlinear family would be a publishable structural discovery; its
   absence, honestly measured, is Bayesian evidence for the conjecture at
   the bottom of the ladder (two-sided reading, lens 6).

3. **Invariant-first (lens 4).** Cheap deciders to try before enumeration
   at each new length: degree cap $2^\tau$; coefficient-height caps per
   gate count; content/primitive-part reduction; parity and 2-adic
   valuation constraints on achievable root sets; the additive-complexity
   subcount (roots bounded via Rojas-type theorems once verified). Each
   invariant that decides a case replaces hours of search.

4. **Reformulation/dictionary (lens 7).** Translations available: real tau
   conjecture (sparse-products world, where quantitative bounds EXIST:
   KPT15), Newton-polygon tau (polygon combinatorics), PosSLP (decision
   twin), Lipton's factoring bridge (records as cryptographic statements),
   addition-chain literature (the +-only classical case with a century of
   technique). Import: canonicalization and search technique from
   addition-chain optimal-search work; export: our census as data for the
   variant conjectures.

5. **Parameter ladder (lens 8).** $z_{\max}(\tau)$ itself; secondary
   ladders: max distinct RATIONAL roots (contrast), max REAL roots
   (contrast where the conjecture is false; Chebyshev mechanism), integer
   roots WITH multiplicity (different growth). Locating where integer
   rigidity diverges from real abundance is the structural question.

6. **Adversarial/audit (lens 10).** Regression gate against Markstroem's
   published integer values before trusting the polynomial enumerator;
   independent double root-counting on every record; hypotheses committed
   before runs.
