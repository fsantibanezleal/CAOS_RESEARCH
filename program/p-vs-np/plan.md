### 3. Rules of this plan

- Every statement is labelled by how it was established, and a derivation is not cited as established before a
  reader other than its writer has reviewed it.
- A result on the ladder is written as a result on the route. No record of this programme states progress on the
  prize statement.

  the path map.

  conditional statement.
