## The pick: `petersen-coloring` (area `combinatorics`, new)

Why this one, in radar terms: the counterexamples are explicit finite objects with public
certificates; every question in the consequence audit is a SAT instance whose negative answer is
a DRAT proof and whose positive answer is an explicit cover checkable by a standard-library
script; controls exist on both sides (the Petersen graph and small snarks are colorable; a
corrupted counterexample must become colorable); two primary papers plus the 2013 and 2019
census papers make the sources complete; the audit surface is unclaimed as of 2026-09-03; the
work runs on the WSL CaDiCaL/drat-trim toolchain already used by the Huneke-Wiegand record.

Why not minimality first: Goedgebeur, Jooken, Macajova, Mattiolo, Mazzuoccolo wrote the snark
generators and pinned `[38, 52]` within six days of the disproof; a CAOS minimality race is a
lottery. Minimality is kept as a bounded, grammar-restricted question (compositions of copies of
the Petersen 4-pole `F` with small connectors below 52 vertices), decided by exact P-coloring-set
calculus, and declared only after the audit lands.

Non-claims, fixed now: discovery priority stays with Putman (112), Goedgebeur et al. (52), the
anonymous 68 poster, and Jooken (human-checkable proof). CAOS does not claim a new counterexample
unless an experiment produces one with a checked certificate. Replication is labeled replication.
