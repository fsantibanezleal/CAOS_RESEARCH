## Problem statement and status

A graph is maximal non-Hamiltonian (MNH) if it is not Hamiltonian but G + e is Hamiltonian for every nonedge e.
**Hoa's conjecture (1994, Problem 266, Discrete Math. 164 (1997)):** if G is MNH and C is a longest cycle of G,
then G - V(C) is complete [V via Zhan]. **Disproved** by Zhan (arXiv:2608.00957v3): counterexamples of every order
n >= 56, with G - V(C) = 2K_1, toughness exactly 1, circumference n - 2 [V]. Zhan makes no claim that 56 is least
and poses Questions 2 (must every component of G - V(C) be complete?), 3 (a connected non-complete residual?) and
4 (f(n), the maximum number of components of G - V(C)) [V]. Hoa proved the conjecture when toughness < 1 or
delta >= n/3 [V via Zhan]. Known: the least counterexample order n_0 lies in [8, 56] (our atlas check to n = 7).

## Why the reference matters

It is a fresh disproof (August 2026) with no follow-up, a wide gap [8, 56] for the least counterexample, three
open questions stated by the author, and a classical object (MNH graphs; OEIS A185306 stuck at n = 10 since 2013).

## Feasibility class and surface

Class A (exact, finite, decidable instance by instance). Exact surface: complete classification of MNH graphs by
order with exhaustive generation (nauty geng) and exact Hamiltonicity; every longest cycle enumerated. Numeric
surface: none.

## Known examples, counterexamples and closest prior work

K_{n-1} plus a pendant edge, two cliques sharing a vertex (connectivity 1), K_k join (k+1)K_1, the
hypohamiltonian MNH graphs (Petersen, Coxeter, Isaacs snarks), all satisfying the conjecture; Zhan's G_56 and its
simplicial blow-ups (counterexamples). Prior enumeration: Jamrozik-Kalinowski-Skupien 1982 catalogue (n <= 10),
OEIS A185306 (to n = 10), Moulton 2014 (unpublished, to n = 20 for a spanning-structure question), the platypus
lists of Goedgebeur-Neyt-Zamfirescu (complete to n = 12; every MNH graph without a universal vertex is a platypus).

## Invariants and lenses

Invariants: circumference, the residual G - V(C) over all longest cycles, its component count and completeness,
toughness, minimum degree, the degree-sum tightness of nonadjacent pairs (d(u) + d(v) <= n - 1, Bondy-Chvatal).
Lenses: (1) exhaustive enumeration by order; (2) constructive gadget search through the Bullock et al. Theorem
3.13 mechanism that Zhan uses (K_1 join a maximal nontraceable graph); (3) structural (toughness and degree
conditions under which Hoa's theorem applies, to prune and to explain).

## Compute, costs and stop conditions

Order <= 11: about 10^9 connected graphs, minutes on 24 cores with a pruning filter in C. Order 12: about
1.6 x 10^11, hours. Stop at order 12 unless a cheaper structured generator is built. The gadget search is
bounded by the classified MNH graphs.

## Novelty risks, publication risks, non-claims

Moulton's unpublished list may already contain the small-order answer [U]. The 1982 catalogue covers n <= 10.
Non-claims: no asymptotic statement; a negative result to order 12 does not lower Zhan's 56 by itself; any
counterexample is verified by an independent implementation before a claim.
