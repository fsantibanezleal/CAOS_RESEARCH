# Scoped operational-record relocation

Declared 2026-10-04 before implementation. Authority: CAOS_MANAGE
`conventions/shared-control-repo.md`, binding 2026-10-03. Only the Riemann
initiative is in scope; scientific inputs and published bytes remain frozen.

- WHEN Riemann operational records are removed from the public product,
  their exact source bytes SHALL first be archived, committed and pushed to
  the private coordination repository, with source commit and per-file hashes.
- The public structure guard SHALL recognize a validated private-location
  declaration while continuing to require every scientific evidence directory
  and file. A malformed declaration SHALL fail.
- Public navigation SHALL resolve relocated operational references to their
  private canonical locations. Mathematical proof/certificate inputs SHALL
  remain at their existing paths, and frontend/data trees SHALL be unchanged.
- The private current status SHALL record the verified release, bounded
  incomplete search and next scientific obligation without promoting partial
  numerical evidence to a theorem.
